# Updating Course Content — Team Guide

**Describe the change. Click Merge. It's live.**

For the Applying AI at Work content team · no coding tools needed

## How it works, in 30 seconds

All course text lives in simple text files in one shared home (GitHub). Canvas
just *displays* it.

1. **You change the text** — through the chat app (or on the GitHub site)
2. **The system rebuilds the page** — automatically, after you approve
3. **Canvas shows the new version** — on its own, within ~10 minutes

That's why we **never edit inside Canvas** — the system would overwrite it on
the next rebuild.

## One-time setup

About 10 minutes, once. No Git, no Python, no terminal, no tokens.

1. **Get added to the repo.** Send your GitHub username to Sathya. No account?
   Create a free one at github.com.
2. **Install one app.** **Claude desktop** or **ChatGPT desktop** — sign in
   with your own account.
3. **Connect it to GitHub.** The first time you use it, the app asks once.
   Approve it and you're set.

✓ That's everything. You're ready to edit.

> **No Canvas token needed.** Canvas access is already configured centrally in
> the publishing system — you never handle Canvas credentials.

## Making a change

The everyday routine — three small steps.

1. **Describe the change in the app.** Name the course, sprint, page, and the
   exact text:

   > "In course 1, Sprint 3, on the 'Stakeholder Map Draft' page, change
   > 'Your submission' to 'Your Canvas submission'."

2. **Review the before & after, then click Merge.** The app gives you a link
   showing exactly what changed. Look it over — that Merge click is your
   approval, and nothing changes until you make it.

3. **It goes live on its own.** The page updates within about 10 minutes, and
   you get a plain confirmation that it worked — or a plain reason it didn't.

> **Tip:** the more specific you are — page title, current text, desired
> text — the better the first draft.

## What you handle vs. what the maintainer handles

**Yours — anytime: the words students read**

wording · instructions · examples · explanations · typos

**Maintainer — send a request: course structure & settings**

new pages · renaming · points · due dates · publish / unpublish · reordering

## Three things to remember

- **Edit the text files, not Canvas.** Changes made inside Canvas get
  overwritten by the system.
- **Content questions go to the team.** The tools faithfully publish whatever
  we decide a page should say.
- **Wondering if it's live?** Ask the app "did my change go live?" — or just
  open the course page and look.

Your focus is the **content**. Everything else is plumbing — and the plumbing
is our job.
