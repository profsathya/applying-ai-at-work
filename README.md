# Applying AI at Work: Build Solutions with a Human Touch

This repository is the design starting point for the "Applying AI at Work" workforce certificate program. It is a partnership between CTI (curriculum and framework) and De Anza College (delivery and accreditation), with intent to scale to additional community colleges. The certificate teaches working professionals to direct AI using the institutional knowledge they already have.

## Who this is for

Working professionals whose roles are being reshaped by AI. They have real jobs, real stakeholders, and existing knowledge of their organizations. This certificate builds AI direction capability on top of that knowledge.

## Two-course structure

- **Course 1: Problem Framing with AI** — Build the capability to take a vague workplace concern and turn it into a clearly framed problem, validated by real stakeholders.
- **Course 2: Building Solutions with AI** — Build the capability to direct AI in solving a framed problem, with the participant providing judgment, context, and accountability.

Each course is 10 weeks: Week 1 orientation + 4 two-week sprints + Week 10 capstone.

## How to navigate this repo

Start with `context/` to understand the design philosophy, audience, and frameworks. Then look at `course1/` and `course2/` for the prescribed course structure and high-level outcomes.

- `context/` — Design principles, frameworks, audience definition, and team reference materials
- `course1/` — Course 1 structure, outcomes, and (eventually) module content
- `course2/` — Course 2 structure, outcomes, and (eventually) module content
- `shared/` — Assets used across both courses, including the sprint HTML template
- `config/` — Delivery platform configuration (once decided)
- `scripts/` — Automation for content management and deployment (once built)

## A note on this repo

This is the design starting point. Content development happens here. This repo does not inherit from or depend on any other course repository. The team will develop module content, sprint themes, weekly briefings, and assessments within this structure.
