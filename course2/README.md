# Course 2: Building Solutions with AI

## Purpose

Build the capability to direct AI in solving a framed problem, with the participant providing judgment, context, and accountability. Where Course 1 teaches problem framing, Course 2 teaches solution building — prototyping, testing, iterating, and demonstrating a working solution that addresses a real organizational need.

## Audience

Working professionals whose roles are being reshaped by AI. See `context/audience.md` for the full audience definition.

## Format

10 weeks, online, asynchronous.

## Structure

- **Week 1:** Orientation — reconnect or onboard, revisit problem framing (or choose a new problem), align on the solution-building arc
- **Weeks 2–9:** Four two-week sprints developing solution-building capability
- **Week 10:** Capstone — demonstrate solution to peers and stakeholders, articulate human contribution

See `structure.md` for the prescribed course shape and `outcomes.md` for high-level learning outcomes.

## Outcome focus

Participants leave this course with a working or demonstrated solution to a real workplace problem, plus the ability to articulate what they contributed that AI could not. They can direct AI as a genuine partner — leveraging its strengths while providing the judgment, context, and accountability that only they can offer.

## Module content

Detailed sprint content is intentionally not in this README. The team will develop sprint themes, activities, assessments, and weekly briefings in `modules/`. The course structure is prescribed; the content within that structure is for the team to design.
