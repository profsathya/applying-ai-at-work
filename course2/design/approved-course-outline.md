## Course ID

CIS 502

## Course Title

Designing Solutions with AI

## Units and Hours

2.5 hrs lecture, 1.5 hrs lab (tentative)

## Department

Computer Science and Information Systems

## Course Description

Building on the problem-solving skills developed in the previous course, students in this course will focus on solutions that address system-level complexity. Students will learn to design whole-system solutions, validate assumptions before building, and integrate insights from multiple domains. Working individually and in teams, students will move from design through prototyping to delivery. The course emphasizes moving beyond simply completing tasks to creating measurable outcomes for stakeholders, a shift that requires human judgment about value, context, and tradeoffs.

## Prerequisite and Advisory

Prerequisite: Reframing Problems with AI

## Mode of Delivery

Asynchronous online with optional synchronous work sessions.

## Course Objectives / SLOs

- Design, validate, and build solutions that address system-level complexity.
- Work and contribute in a team to build solutions with measurable outcomes.
- Reflect on and articulate human contribution to the solution.

## Course Outline

- Design a system level solution.
  - Address stakeholder tradeoffs and constraints
  - Technical implementation
  - Integrate cross-domain insights from other related fields
  - Address system-level tradeoffs
- Validate assumptions before building.
  - Identify critical assumptions that could undermine solutions
  - Create prototypes of solution components
  - Pivot based on evidence from prototypes
- Build and deliver solution.
  - Focus on measurable outcomes over simple outputs
  - Demonstrate measurable outcomes to stakeholders
  - Communicate the full progression from problem discovery to value delivery
- Work collaboratively while maintaining individual accountability
  - Contribute to a team-based solution
  - Document personal growth in integrative thinking and adaptive building capabilities
- Reflect on and articulate human contribution:
  - Explain how human insights identified which problems needed to be solved
  - Document how stakeholder needs are balanced through tradeoffs
  - Identify the cross-domain synthesis that came from human pattern recognition

## Lab Outline

- Review integrated problem-solving document with AI partner to identify stakeholder perspectives and map constraints and resources
- Integrate insights from multiple domains, address system tradeoffs, and document optimization. Work with peer to challenge each other's architectural choices.
- **Deliverable: Draft of solution design**
- Identify assumptions and categorize them on their risks to the solution, design tests for the top 3-5 most critical assumptions.
- Validate top assumptions with prototypes, gather evidence and results, and confirm or reject assumptions. Pivot based on results.
- **Deliverable: Report on assumption validation**
- Plan and design components of solution. Map each component to stakeholder needs and purpose of solution. Define outcomes for each component.
  - Optional: Collaborate with team to plan comprehensive team solution and integrate teamwork.
- Build components to address the solution purpose and obtain validation from stakeholders.
  - Optional: integrate components with team.
- Demos and feedback from peer or team and from stakeholders.
- Finalize working solution or functional demonstration, document progression
- **Deliverable: Working solution or functional demonstration**
- Develop portfolio by gathering evidence to show problem-to-value progression and measurable outcomes to stakeholders.
- Document human value contribution: judgment calls in solution architecture, navigation through stakeholder conflicts, cross domain insights, and human synthesis.
- **Deliverable: Portfolio and reflection**

## Assignments

- Practice synthesizing solution steps with AI and record the problem-solving sessions.
- Peer review or team review and collaboration.
- Four documents on progress from problem framing to integrated problem solving goals

## Methods of Evaluation

- Problem solving session will be evaluated for completeness and genuine engagement with the framework.
- Peer review or team collaboration will be evaluated for active participation and team work.
- The deliverables will be evaluated for evidence of system-level thinking, measurable outcomes, and articulation of human judgment and contribution.
