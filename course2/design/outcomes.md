# Course 2 Outcomes

High-level outcomes for Course 2: Building Solutions with AI. These describe what a completer can do.

- Direct AI to build or prototype solutions to framed workplace problems, providing the judgment, context, and organizational knowledge that AI cannot supply.
- Test assumptions and pivot based on evidence, using an iterative build-test-learn cycle rather than committing to a single plan.
- Demonstrate working or prototyped solutions to real stakeholders, incorporating their feedback and adjusting the approach based on what they learn.
- Articulate the human contribution to the solution — what they provided (judgment, context, stakeholder relationships, accountability) that AI could not, and why that contribution mattered.

How these outcomes are taught and assessed is for the team to design.
