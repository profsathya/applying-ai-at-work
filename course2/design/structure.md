# Course 2 Structure

## Week 1 — Orientation

Participants reconnect with their cohort (or onboard if new), revisit their problem framing from Course 1 (or choose a new problem if taking Course 2 independently), and align on the solution-building arc ahead. This is the autonomy moment for Course 2: participants decide what solution they will build or prototype and confirm that their problem framing is ready for solution work.

Specific orientation activities are for the team to design.

## Sprints 1–4 (Weeks 2–9, two weeks each)

Each sprint develops the participant's capability to direct AI in building or prototyping a solution to their framed problem. Across the four sprints, participants move from initial solution concepts toward a working or demonstrated solution validated by real stakeholders.

Specific sprint themes, activities, and assessments are for the team to design. The following constraints apply to all sprints:

- Each sprint should include a real stakeholder touchpoint — an actual conversation with someone in the participant's organization, not a simulated interaction.
- Each sprint should include a visible AI partnership moment — a structured activity where the human and AI contributions are distinct and identifiable.
- Each sprint should produce a visible artifact that demonstrates growing capability.

## Week 10 — Capstone

Participants demonstrate their solution to peers and stakeholders, and articulate the human contribution — what they provided that AI could not. This is the competence moment for Course 2: visible evidence of what they can now do, and clear articulation of the human-AI partnership that produced the result.

Specific capstone format is for the team to design.

---

This structure is prescribed because it is load-bearing for the audience and SDT design. Working professionals need orientation before being asked to produce, a steady sprint cadence that respects their time constraints, and a capstone that makes their growth visible. The content within each sprint is for the team to develop.
