# Why This Is Archived

This is the V2 rebuild of Sprint 4, "Close the Learning Gap." It lived at
`course1/sprints/sprint-8/` and appeared on the course homepage as sprint 8,
tagged "Learning Gap V2." It was never published to Canvas: every artifact
carried `publish: false`, and no manifest or deployment state referenced it.

It was retired on 2026-08-30 and replaced by the third build of the sprint,
which now carries the "Sprint 4: Close The Learning Gap (V2)" title and lives
at `course1/sprints/sprint-9/`. That build starts from the gap the Sprint 3
stakeholder conversation opens up, splits gap naming into its own graded step
(Name the Gap), adds an AI Exchange that pressure-tests the committed gap
before any planning, and rebalances points across the module.

The six artifacts kept here:

- `sprint-4-close-the-learning-gap-v2.md`: module header
- `introduction-close-the-learning-gap-v2.md`: page
- `sprint-4-concept-check-v2.md`: quiz, 5 points
- `dojo-lab-design-the-learning-path-v2.md`: assignment, 35 points
- `learn-and-verify-v2.md`: discussion, 50 points
- `sprint-4-reflection-what-i-learned-and-how-i-know-v2.md`: quiz, 10 points

Kept here as reference for:

- The learning-path framing and rubric language before the rebuild
- Comparing what the third build changed and why
- Recovering any wording worth carrying forward

Do not edit or publish these files. They sit outside `course*/sprints/`, so
the schema validator and the Canvas publish workflow both skip them. If any
of this content should come back, copy it into a live sprint directory with a
fresh slug and artifact id rather than moving these files back.
