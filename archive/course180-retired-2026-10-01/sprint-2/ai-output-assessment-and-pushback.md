# AI Output Assessment and Pushback

## Purpose

Practice treating AI output as material to assess, not a final answer. You will critique one AI-generated set of options and improve it through pushback.

## Task Instructions

1. Choose one AI output from your 3Cs exploration that offered options, recommendations, or a revised frame.
2. Read it as a responsible decision-maker. Mark what is useful, generic, wrong, risky, or unverified.
3. Push back with a follow-up prompt that adds context, challenges an assumption, or asks for clearer tradeoffs.
4. Compare the first and second outputs.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- What AI suggested.
- What was useful.
- What was generic or wrong.
- What you challenged.
- How the second output changed.
- What still cannot be trusted without evidence.

## Submission Format

Text entry in Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your submission should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

Add both AI outputs, your critique, and your evidence limits to the course portfolio.
