# AI Exploration Log and 3Cs Application Log

## Purpose

Create a durable record of how you directed AI, assessed output, and decided what needs confirmation.

## Task Instructions

1. Gather your prompts, Dojo outputs, pushback notes, and peer exchange insights.
2. Organize the work around Context, Choices, and Confirmation.
3. Revise your selected direction or problem frame if the exploration changed your thinking.
4. Be explicit about what you contributed that AI could not.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- Reframed problem.
- Context provided to AI.
- Prompt iterations.
- AI-generated choices.
- Assumptions surfaced.
- Learner assessment of each option.
- Confirmation plan.
- Selected direction or revised frame.
- What you contributed that AI could not.

## Submission Format

Text entry in Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your submission should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

This log carries directly into Sprint 3. Your confirmation plan should point toward real stakeholder evidence.
