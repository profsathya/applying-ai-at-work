# Sprint 2: Direct AI Deliberately

In this sprint, you learn to direct AI with context, choices, and confirmation. You are not collecting plausible suggestions. You are using AI to help you think through options, assumptions, and evidence needs more clearly.

This sprint is part of Reframing Problems with AI. You will keep using the same real current problem throughout the course. A workplace problem is ideal, but the problem can also come from a job search, role transition, volunteer role, community work, caregiving or family system, campus work, professional portfolio, small business, or another real setting where actual people are affected.

By the end of this sprint, you should be able to:

1. Provide AI with context, constraints, audience, and purpose.
2. Use the 3Cs to surface choices and confirmation needs.
3. Assess AI output before trusting or using it.
4. Document prompt iterations, pushback, decisions, and remaining uncertainty.

## Sprint Rhythm

1. Bring forward your reframed problem from Sprint 1.
2. Read the concept page on Context, Choices, and Confirmation.
3. Run one structured Dojo exploration round and capture the result.
4. Critique one AI output and push back to improve it.
5. Submit your AI Exploration Log and 3Cs Application Log.

## Portfolio Thread

Carry forward the work from this sprint into your living course portfolio. Keep the artifacts, AI prompts, pushback notes, stakeholder evidence, and reflections that show how your thinking changed.

## Completion Criteria

You are done when you can show how your direction changed the AI output and what still needs real evidence before you trust any direction.
