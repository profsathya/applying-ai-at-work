# Integrated Problem Document Draft

## Purpose

Create a complete draft of the final document so you can test whether the argument holds together before the final submission.

## Task Instructions

1. Use your synthesis audit to choose evidence from prior artifacts.
2. Write for an outside reader who does not already know your course journey.
3. Make gaps visible instead of hiding them.
4. Flag the section where you most want peer feedback.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- Current problem statement.
- Why the problem matters.
- Scope and boundaries.
- Stakeholder perspectives.
- Context, choices, and confirmation.
- Learning gap and what was learned.
- Problem-solving goals.
- Evidence and remaining uncertainty.
- Human and AI contribution split.
- Readiness claim.

## Submission Format

Text entry in Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your submission should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

This draft is the working version you will revise after peer review.
