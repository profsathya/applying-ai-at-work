# Course 1: Reframing Problems with AI

## Purpose

Reframing Problems with AI is CIS 501, the first course in Applying AI at Work: Build Solutions with a Human Touch. Participants learn to work with AI as a cognitive partner while keeping human judgment in the lead. They move from a vague goal or surface request to a validated, scoped, stakeholder-aware problem that is ready to carry forward into later solution-building work.

The course is not about producing polished AI outputs. It is about making thinking visible, testing assumptions, integrating people and context, and developing enough self-directed learning capacity to move a real problem forward.

## Audience

Adult learners and working professionals whose roles, goals, or communities are being reshaped by AI. Some participants will have a current job and some will not. Every participant works on a real current problem connected to actual people. Acceptable contexts include work, job search, promotion or role change, volunteer work, community work, caregiving or family systems, campus work, professional portfolio development, small business work, or another real setting where actual people are affected.

## Format

10 weeks, online, asynchronous, with optional synchronous work sessions.

## Structure

- **Sprint 1, Weeks 1-2:** Frame What Is Worth Solving
- **Sprint 2, Weeks 3-4:** Direct AI Deliberately
- **Sprint 3, Weeks 5-6:** Integrate People And Context
- **Sprint 4, Weeks 7-8:** Close The Learning Gap
- **Sprint 5, Weeks 9-10:** Synthesize And Show Readiness

Each sprint uses local Markdown artifacts under `course1/sprints/sprint-<n>/`. The Canvas production manifest remains the source for Canvas course identity and deployment state.

## Outcome Focus

Participants leave CIS 501 able to take a real goal or vague concern and turn it into a clearly framed problem with stakeholder validation, AI exploration, targeted learning, and a defensible readiness judgment. They can explain what AI contributed and what they contributed that AI alone could not provide.
