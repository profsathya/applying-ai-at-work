**Material build decisions:**

- **Storage.** The handoff's `deanza/course1/activities/*.html` and `progress-map.json` paths describe generated Common-Curriculum output. Markdown is the source of truth here and generated output is never hand-edited, so the module was built at `course1/sprints/sprint-8/`, which already held the Sprint 4 V3 artifacts the handoff's file mapping names, and `course1/homepage.yaml` took the role of the progress map.
- **Module title.** `Sprint 4: Close the Learning Gap (V3)`, per the handoff's own recommendation to match Sprint 3, replacing the `(V2)` string that the existing artifacts carried in both `title` and `module`. This resolves the handoff's open item 4 as a proposal, not as an approval.
- **Slugs and identity.** Every existing slug and `artifact_id` was preserved, including `dojo-lab-design-the-learning-path-v3` under its new title, as the handoff requires and as repository policy requires of `artifact_id`. The two new artifacts take `course1-sprints-sprint-8-<slug>`; the five existing ones carry a `course1-sprints-sprint-9-` prefix that predates this build and cannot be changed.
- **Transcript block.** The Dojo Lab body ends at "State your decisions before requesting the transcript." The handoff's "Submit the complete transcript", its transcript request, the CONTINUED steps, the response box, and the "Own your progress" line are all emitted by `dojo_submission: {mode: transcript, prompt_version: v1}`, from the approved registry text. Pasting them into the body would have rendered each one twice.
- **Presentation.** The Introduction uses `page_presentation: reading`, as Sprint 3 V3 does, which suppresses the goal panel. Every other artifact that renders a goal panel received a real `learning_goal` drawn from its own approved copy, replacing the renderer's circular default. No illustrations were added: the handoff supplies no image direction, and the pages are worked examples, sorted lists, and prompts where a picture would add nothing.
- **Retirement.** The three replaced artifacts were moved unchanged to `course1/retired/2026-09-21/sprint-8/`, following the `2026-09-11` precedent, rather than deleted. Their bytes are identical to the fingerprints recorded in `20260911T170706Z-v2-consistency.md`.
