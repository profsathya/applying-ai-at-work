# How this assignment is assessed

CIS 501 · Sprint 3 · Stakeholder Conversation · 50 points · Main Assignments

Use the self-check to review your report and updated map before submitting. Each line describes full-credit evidence. Your instructor checks the evidence; checking a box does not award points.

## Self-check

- [ ] Section A carries over the stakeholder, applicable relationships, assumption, and starting Problem Frame from the map, with the actual conversation format, date, and time. **5 points**
- [ ] Three questions build on the map and ask this stakeholder about specific experiences, decisions, or tradeoffs while leaving the answer open. **6 points**
- [ ] Each question has a learning statement explaining what its answer could reveal about the assumption being tested. **4 points**
- [ ] The Part 1 JSON records the required exchange about the participant's draft questions and their use of AI follow-up questions to examine leading, vague, or missing-evidence wording. **5 points**
- [ ] Section B records the real stakeholder's account closely enough to support later conclusions, with sensitive material paraphrased and observations kept separate from interpretation. **10 points**
- [ ] Section C identifies what was confirmed or complicated, the initial effect on the frame, and a next question, with evidence or uncertainty behind those impressions. **5 points**
- [ ] Section D explains the revised or retained Problem Frame using conversation evidence, distinguishes what was confirmed from what remains inferred, and identifies what still needs validation. **8 points**
- [ ] The updated map reflects what the conversation settled or corrected, keeps unresolved fields inferred, adds stakeholders if the conversation revealed them, and retains the earlier Dojo paragraph. **5 points**
- [ ] The report identifies the participant and keeps Sections A to D recognizable; the supplied files are readable and can be associated with that report. **2 points**

## What makes it strong

The assumption in Section A leads to questions that the chosen stakeholder can answer. The account in Section B supports the conclusions in Sections C and D, and the updated map reflects those conclusions. The work shows what became clearer and what still needs validation. Confirmation, revision, and justified uncertainty can each be strong results.

## Grading and next use

Your instructor assesses the submitted evidence and reasoning using these criteria. The work is worth 50 points in Main Assignments, which counts toward your final grade. AI feedback supports your question review; the instructor determines your grade. Optional row feedback earns no points.

Use the feedback and the remaining uncertainties in Section D when you identify your learning gaps in Sprint 4. A clear, evidence-supported decision to retain the original frame can earn full credit.

Submit the existing three files: the completed report, the updated Stakeholder Map, and the Part 1 AI exchange JSON. In this local prototype, the report download also includes your self-check states. The self-check is part of the report, not a fourth file.
