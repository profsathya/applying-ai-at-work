# CIS 501 instructor rubric: Stakeholder Conversation

**Local draft.** Nine items total 50 points. Participant-facing content is in participant-self-check.md; the partial-credit ladder and internal priorities stay in this instructor record.

## Proposed scoring

Score each item once: 5 = full evidence, 3 = partial evidence, 1 = minimal evidence, 0 = no assessable evidence. Item points = maximum points × rating ÷ 5. Sum before rounding; display one decimal place. The point conversion and zero are proposed CIS 501 adaptations, rather than a scoring formula verified in Sathya's courses.

Judge the quality of the investigation relative to the starting frame. A confirmed frame, a revised frame, or justified uncertainty can earn full credit. Do not require an AI error, a changed belief, a discovered surprise, or a proposed AI solution. Do not grade prose polish, AI agreement, or use of optional row feedback.

Grade the final questions only under item 2; item 3 assesses their investigative purpose and item 4 assesses the required exchange process. Item 8 assesses the conversation-driven map updates, not the original map already graded separately. Item 9 assesses identification and readability, not a second penalty for a missing content file. A missing file leaves its own substantive evidence unassessable; do not repeat that deduction elsewhere for the same omission.

## Criterion ladder

### 1. Starting point and stakeholder (5 points)

Evidence: Report Section A; existing Stakeholder Map and starting Problem Frame. Internal feedback priority: 7 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Contextual problem framing.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 5 | Section A carries over the stakeholder, applicable relationships, assumption, and starting Problem Frame from the map, with the actual conversation format, date, and time. |
| 3 | 3 | Section A includes the starting frame and stakeholder, but a relationship, carried-over assumption, or conversation detail is missing or inconsistent. Complete that detail from the map or the actual conversation. |
| 1 | 1 | Section A identifies a stakeholder or a starting frame, but the assumption and the stakeholder's relationship to it cannot be followed. Connect this conversation to the assumption already in the map. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable preparation record is supplied. |

### 2. Three questions that seek evidence (6 points)

Evidence: Report: Your three validation questions. Internal feedback priority: 5 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Evidence-seeking inquiry.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 6 | Three questions build on the map and ask this stakeholder about specific experiences, decisions, or tradeoffs while leaving the answer open. |
| 3 | 3.6 | The questions include useful requests for experience or evidence, but one is missing, vague, leading, or directed at something this stakeholder cannot answer. Revise that question so this person can describe what actually happened. |
| 1 | 1.2 | At least one question is present, but the set mostly seeks opinions or approval of a solution. Use the map to ask about an experience, decision, or tradeoff this person can describe. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable validation questions are supplied. |

### 3. What each question could establish (4 points)

Evidence: Report: three What I hope to learn responses. Internal feedback priority: 6 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Purposeful investigation.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 4 | Each question has a learning statement explaining what its answer could reveal about the assumption being tested. |
| 3 | 2.4 | Some learning statements explain what an answer could establish, but another is missing or repeats the question without connecting it to the assumption. Explain what that answer would help you determine. |
| 1 | 0.8 | The learning statements mainly describe getting agreement or collecting general information. Connect the information sought to the assumption this conversation tests. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable learning statements are supplied. |

### 4. Question review with AI (5 points)

Evidence: Required Part 1 AI exchange JSON; final question table. Internal feedback priority: 8 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Human direction of AI.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 5 | The Part 1 JSON records the required exchange about the participant's draft questions and their use of AI follow-up questions to examine leading, vague, or missing-evidence wording. |
| 3 | 3 | The exchange is about the participant's draft, but the diagnostic discussion is incomplete. Use the follow-up questions to examine the specific wording issue and retain that exchange record. |
| 1 | 1 | An exchange file is present, but it shows little examination of the draft questions or cannot be connected to the final table. Use the diagnostic follow-up questions to examine your own wording. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable record of the required question exchange is supplied. |

### 5. Record of the real conversation (10 points)

Evidence: Report Section B; actual conversation details in Section A. Internal feedback priority: 2 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Stakeholder evidence.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 10 | Section B records the real stakeholder's account closely enough to support later conclusions, with sensitive material paraphrased and observations kept separate from interpretation. |
| 3 | 6 | A real account is recorded, but general summaries or mixed-in interpretation make some later claims hard to check. Identify what the stakeholder actually said or described, using a privacy-safe paraphrase where needed. |
| 1 | 2 | The notes mainly state the participant's conclusions or a generic account, with little identifiable stakeholder evidence. Record the specific experience, decision, or tradeoff the stakeholder described. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable evidence from a real stakeholder conversation is supplied. |

### 6. Immediate interpretation and next question (5 points)

Evidence: Report Section C. Internal feedback priority: 4 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Reflective evidence interpretation.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 5 | Section C identifies what was confirmed or complicated, the initial effect on the frame, and a next question, with evidence or uncertainty behind those impressions. |
| 3 | 3 | Section C includes an initial reading, but an impression lacks an evidence link or one requested response is missing. Point to the conversation detail behind the impression and complete the next question. |
| 1 | 1 | Section C gives a reaction with little connection to the conversation or what still needs investigation. Identify what you heard and what question it leaves you with. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable first-impression responses are supplied. |

### 7. Evidence-supported Problem Frame (8 points)

Evidence: Report Section D, checked against Sections A and B. Internal feedback priority: 1 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Human judgment under uncertainty.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 8 | Section D explains the revised or retained Problem Frame using conversation evidence, distinguishes what was confirmed from what remains inferred, and identifies what still needs validation. |
| 3 | 4.8 | Section D offers a plausible conclusion, but a frame decision or confirmed claim lacks support in the conversation, or an important uncertainty is omitted. Connect that decision to the evidence and identify the limit of what it establishes. |
| 1 | 1.6 | Section D asserts a changed or unchanged frame with little evidence, or treats an inference as confirmed. Explain which conversation detail supports the conclusion and what remains uncertain. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable concluding Problem Frame judgment is supplied. |

### 8. Evidence carried into the map (5 points)

Evidence: Updated Stakeholder Map, checked against report Section D. Internal feedback priority: 3 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Evidence-informed revision.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 5 | The updated map reflects what the conversation settled or corrected, keeps unresolved fields inferred, adds stakeholders if the conversation revealed them, and retains the earlier Dojo paragraph. |
| 3 | 3 | The map contains relevant updates, but a settled field, incorrect claim, or status conflicts with Section D. Align that field and its reason with what the conversation established. |
| 1 | 1 | The map is present, but few updates can be traced to the conversation, or claims are marked confirmed without support. Update the specific fields the conversation settled and retain uncertainty elsewhere. |
| 0 | 0 | No assessable updated map is supplied. |

### 9. Usable submission (2 points)

Evidence: Report, updated map, and Part 1 AI JSON as the existing three-file submission. Internal feedback priority: 9 (1 is highest). CIS 501 capability tag: Communicating assessable work.

| Rating | Award | Observable evidence and feedback |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 5 | 2 | The report identifies the participant and keeps Sections A to D recognizable; the supplied files are readable and can be associated with that report. |
| 3 | 1.2 | The supplied work is readable, but the participant's name or a section label is missing, or a file is difficult to associate with the report. Make that identification clear. |
| 1 | 0.4 | Some supplied work is readable, but missing identification or unclear organization makes it difficult to follow. Identify the participant and preserve the report's section labels. |
| 0 | 0 | No usable submission package is supplied. |

## Feedback

After scoring, select one or two highest-priority items with incomplete evidence. Quote or identify the exact place in the participant's submission, use the relevant ladder text as the nudge, and ask one focused question. Leave the participant to supply the evidence and decide the revision. Capability tags use the same item scores; do not create a second grade for the tags.

**Illustrative feedback, not a participant quotation:** “Section D treats the handover delay as confirmed for the whole team, while Section B records only the account lead's experience. Which part of that account establishes the wider claim, and what still needs validation?”

No new interview, peer review, word count, late penalty, or submission is added to this assignment. This draft does not define an exception policy for a conversation that has not yet occurred.
