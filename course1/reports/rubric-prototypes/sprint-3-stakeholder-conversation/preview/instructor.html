CIS 501 · Sprint 3 · Main Assignments

Stakeholder Conversation · 50-point rubric

Score the submitted evidence once per item. The participant sees the full-credit self-check; the partial-credit descriptions and priorities belong to this instructor record.

Proposed conversion: 5 = full points, 3 = 60%, 1 = 20%, 0 = no assessable evidence. Confirmation, revision, and justified uncertainty can earn full credit.

No participant work is loaded.

0.0 / 50

1. Starting point and stakeholder · 5 pts

Evidence: Report Section A; existing Stakeholder Map and starting Problem Frame. Feedback priority: 7. Capability: Contextual problem framing.

5 · 5 pts

Section A carries over the stakeholder, applicable relationships, assumption, and starting Problem Frame from the map, with the actual conversation format, date, and time.

3 · 3 pts

Section A includes the starting frame and stakeholder, but a relationship, carried-over assumption, or conversation detail is missing or inconsistent. Complete that detail from the map or the actual conversation.

1 · 1 pts

Section A identifies a stakeholder or a starting frame, but the assumption and the stakeholder's relationship to it cannot be followed. Connect this conversation to the assumption already in the map.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable preparation record is supplied.

2. Three questions that seek evidence · 6 pts

Evidence: Report: Your three validation questions. Feedback priority: 5. Capability: Evidence-seeking inquiry.

5 · 6 pts

Three questions build on the map and ask this stakeholder about specific experiences, decisions, or tradeoffs while leaving the answer open.

3 · 3.6 pts

The questions include useful requests for experience or evidence, but one is missing, vague, leading, or directed at something this stakeholder cannot answer. Revise that question so this person can describe what actually happened.

1 · 1.2 pts

At least one question is present, but the set mostly seeks opinions or approval of a solution. Use the map to ask about an experience, decision, or tradeoff this person can describe.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable validation questions are supplied.

3. What each question could establish · 4 pts

Evidence: Report: three What I hope to learn responses. Feedback priority: 6. Capability: Purposeful investigation.

5 · 4 pts

Each question has a learning statement explaining what its answer could reveal about the assumption being tested.

3 · 2.4 pts

Some learning statements explain what an answer could establish, but another is missing or repeats the question without connecting it to the assumption. Explain what that answer would help you determine.

1 · 0.8 pts

The learning statements mainly describe getting agreement or collecting general information. Connect the information sought to the assumption this conversation tests.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable learning statements are supplied.

4. Question review with AI · 5 pts

Evidence: Required Part 1 AI exchange JSON; final question table. Feedback priority: 8. Capability: Human direction of AI.

5 · 5 pts

The Part 1 JSON records the required exchange about the participant's draft questions and their use of AI follow-up questions to examine leading, vague, or missing-evidence wording.

3 · 3 pts

The exchange is about the participant's draft, but the diagnostic discussion is incomplete. Use the follow-up questions to examine the specific wording issue and retain that exchange record.

1 · 1 pts

An exchange file is present, but it shows little examination of the draft questions or cannot be connected to the final table. Use the diagnostic follow-up questions to examine your own wording.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable record of the required question exchange is supplied.

5. Record of the real conversation · 10 pts

Evidence: Report Section B; actual conversation details in Section A. Feedback priority: 2. Capability: Stakeholder evidence.

5 · 10 pts

Section B records the real stakeholder's account closely enough to support later conclusions, with sensitive material paraphrased and observations kept separate from interpretation.

3 · 6 pts

A real account is recorded, but general summaries or mixed-in interpretation make some later claims hard to check. Identify what the stakeholder actually said or described, using a privacy-safe paraphrase where needed.

1 · 2 pts

The notes mainly state the participant's conclusions or a generic account, with little identifiable stakeholder evidence. Record the specific experience, decision, or tradeoff the stakeholder described.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable evidence from a real stakeholder conversation is supplied.

6. Immediate interpretation and next question · 5 pts

Evidence: Report Section C. Feedback priority: 4. Capability: Reflective evidence interpretation.

5 · 5 pts

Section C identifies what was confirmed or complicated, the initial effect on the frame, and a next question, with evidence or uncertainty behind those impressions.

3 · 3 pts

Section C includes an initial reading, but an impression lacks an evidence link or one requested response is missing. Point to the conversation detail behind the impression and complete the next question.

1 · 1 pts

Section C gives a reaction with little connection to the conversation or what still needs investigation. Identify what you heard and what question it leaves you with.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable first-impression responses are supplied.

7. Evidence-supported Problem Frame · 8 pts

Evidence: Report Section D, checked against Sections A and B. Feedback priority: 1. Capability: Human judgment under uncertainty.

5 · 8 pts

Section D explains the revised or retained Problem Frame using conversation evidence, distinguishes what was confirmed from what remains inferred, and identifies what still needs validation.

3 · 4.8 pts

Section D offers a plausible conclusion, but a frame decision or confirmed claim lacks support in the conversation, or an important uncertainty is omitted. Connect that decision to the evidence and identify the limit of what it establishes.

1 · 1.6 pts

Section D asserts a changed or unchanged frame with little evidence, or treats an inference as confirmed. Explain which conversation detail supports the conclusion and what remains uncertain.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable concluding Problem Frame judgment is supplied.

8. Evidence carried into the map · 5 pts

Evidence: Updated Stakeholder Map, checked against report Section D. Feedback priority: 3. Capability: Evidence-informed revision.

5 · 5 pts

The updated map reflects what the conversation settled or corrected, keeps unresolved fields inferred, adds stakeholders if the conversation revealed them, and retains the earlier Dojo paragraph.

3 · 3 pts

The map contains relevant updates, but a settled field, incorrect claim, or status conflicts with Section D. Align that field and its reason with what the conversation established.

1 · 1 pts

The map is present, but few updates can be traced to the conversation, or claims are marked confirmed without support. Update the specific fields the conversation settled and retain uncertainty elsewhere.

0 · 0 pts: No assessable updated map is supplied.

9. Usable submission · 2 pts

Evidence: Report, updated map, and Part 1 AI JSON as the existing three-file submission. Feedback priority: 9. Capability: Communicating assessable work.

5 · 2 pts

The report identifies the participant and keeps Sections A to D recognizable; the supplied files are readable and can be associated with that report.

3 · 1.2 pts

The supplied work is readable, but the participant's name or a section label is missing, or a file is difficult to associate with the report. Make that identification clear.

1 · 0.4 pts

Some supplied work is readable, but missing identification or unclear organization makes it difficult to follow. Identify the participant and preserve the report's section labels.

0 · 0 pts: No usable submission package is supplied.

Focus the feedback

Address one or two highest-priority items with incomplete evidence. Identify the relevant place in the submission, use the matching rung as the nudge, and ask one focused question. The participant supplies the evidence and decides the revision.

    AI agreement, polished wording, optional feedback use, and a forced change of belief earn no additional points. Item 9 concerns readability and identification; do not repeat a deduction for a missing content file.
