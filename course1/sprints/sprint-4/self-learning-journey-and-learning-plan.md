# Self-Learning Journey and Learning Plan

## Purpose

Document what you chose to learn, how you learned it, how you checked it, and how it changes readiness for the problem.

## Task Instructions

1. Review your gap inventory, Dojo learning path, peer feedback, and verification notes.
2. Use AI to support learning, but include sources beyond AI where the claim matters.
3. Explain what changed in your understanding and how that affects the problem frame.
4. Name what remains uncertain.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- Problem and current frame.
- Knowledge or skill gap.
- Why this gap matters.
- Learning goals.
- Learning path and sources.
- AI prompts used for learning.
- Verification steps.
- What changed in your understanding.
- How the learning changes the problem frame or readiness.
- What remains uncertain.

## Submission Format

Text entry in Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your submission should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

This artifact becomes the learning evidence section of your final readiness report.
