# Peer Exchange: What Are You Choosing Not To Learn?

## Purpose

Use peer review to test your strategic exclusions. Focus is valuable, but every exclusion creates some risk.

## Initial Post

Post:

1. One thing you are choosing not to learn yet.
2. Why you are setting it aside.
3. What risk that choice creates.

## Replies

Reply to at least two peers. In each reply, address:

1. Is that exclusion safe for this stage.
2. What risk it creates or reduces.
3. What minimum check would reduce that risk.

## Discussion Standard

Be concrete and respectful. Help the other person see what is clear, what still sounds like an assumption, and what would make the work stronger. Do not solve their problem for them.
