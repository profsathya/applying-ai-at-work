# Start Here: Close The Learning Gap

## Why This Sprint Matters

After stakeholder validation, you likely know more about what you do not yet understand. This sprint turns that gap into a focused learning path. You will use AI to support learning, but you will verify important claims and decide what is outside scope for now.

You will use AI as a thinking partner, but you remain responsible for the goal, standards, choices, and final meaning of the work. Start with your own thinking, use AI to expand and test it, then decide what to keep, reject, revise, or verify.

## What You Will Do

1. Inventory what you know and what you need to learn.
2. Design a focused learning path with AI and other sources.
3. Choose what not to learn yet and explain the risk.
4. Submit a Self-Learning Journey and Learning Plan tied to your problem.

## Two-Week Sprint Map

This sprint spans two course weeks. Use these labels for pacing; Canvas keeps the work in one module.

- Sprint Week 1: Start here, read the concept page, complete the gap inventory, and use the Dojo to design the learning path.
- Sprint Week 2: Complete the AI peer exchange, submit the Self-Learning Journey and Learning Plan, complete the AI self-check, and refine the AI-guided reflection.

## Time Guidance

Plan for focused work across two weeks. Short concept reading, Dojo practice, peer exchange, and a final artifact should all serve the same real problem rather than becoming separate assignments.

## Working Standard

Specific and honest work is better than polished generic work. Show what you tried, what changed, what evidence you have, and what remains uncertain.

## Carry Forward

Keep the gap inventory, learning path, sources, AI prompts, verification notes, and strategic exclusions. These will support your final readiness judgment.
