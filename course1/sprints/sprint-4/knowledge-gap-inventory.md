# Knowledge Gap Inventory

## Purpose

Identify what you need to learn next, what you already understand well enough, and what you can safely ignore for now.

## Task Instructions

1. Review your stakeholder validation report and revised Goal Plan.
2. List possible knowledge or skill gaps that affect the problem.
3. Separate essential learning from optional learning.
4. Name sources that could help you learn or verify the gap.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- What you understand well enough.
- What you do not understand yet.
- What would change your problem frame if you learned it.
- What you can safely ignore for now.
- What sources could help: AI, documentation, articles, stakeholder conversation, observation, data, expert, or peer.

## Submission Format

Text entry in Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your submission should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

This inventory becomes the input for your Dojo learning path design.
