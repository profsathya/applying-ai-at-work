# Sprint 4 Reflection: What I Learned And How I Know

## Purpose

Reflect on how your learning changed the problem and what you are still not ready to claim.

## Task Instructions

1. Review your learning path, sources, AI prompts, and verification notes.
2. Write an initial reflection in the activity about enough learning for this stage, not complete mastery.
3. Generate AI follow-up questions and use them to identify what is still vague, generic, or unsupported.
4. Revise your reflection so it separates what you know, what you checked, and what remains uncertain.
5. Use the deeper AI guidance button if your answer overclaims what you learned.
6. Save the refined response in the activity.

## Deliverable

Your refined reflection should answer:

- What you learned.
- How you know you learned it well enough for this course stage.
- What AI explained well.
- What you had to verify elsewhere.
- What you are still not ready to claim.

## Submission Format

Download the JSON response file from the activity and upload it to Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your final response should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

Save this reflection as evidence for your final readiness judgment.
