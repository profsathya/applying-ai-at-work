# Sprint 4: Close The Learning Gap

In this sprint, you identify what you need to learn to move the problem forward. AI can accelerate learning, but you decide what matters, what to verify, and what to set aside.

This sprint is part of Reframing Problems with AI. You will keep using the same real current problem throughout the course. A workplace problem is ideal, but the problem can also come from a job search, role transition, volunteer role, community work, caregiving or family system, campus work, professional portfolio, small business, or another real setting where actual people are affected.

By the end of this sprint, you should be able to:

1. Name knowledge or skill gaps connected to the problem.
2. Build a focused learning path with AI and non-AI sources.
3. Verify important claims before using them in your problem frame.
4. Explain what you learned, what changed, and what remains uncertain.

## Sprint Rhythm

1. Inventory what you know, what you do not know, and what you can ignore for now.
2. Read the concept page on self-directed learning with AI.
3. Use the Dojo as Learning Coach and Reflector to focus your learning path.
4. Exchange strategic exclusions with peers.
5. Submit your Self-Learning Journey and Learning Plan.

## Portfolio Thread

Carry forward the work from this sprint into your living course portfolio. Keep the artifacts, AI prompts, pushback notes, stakeholder evidence, and reflections that show how your thinking changed.

## Completion Criteria

You are done when your learning path is focused, verified enough for this stage, and clearly connected to readiness for the problem.
