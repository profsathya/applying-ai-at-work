# Welcome and How This Course Works

This is a 10-week course built around a single problem: one you bring from your own workplace. You will frame it, test it with real stakeholders, sharpen it, and present it. Everything in the course connects back to that problem.

If you have not yet identified your workplace problem, that is the first thing you will do this week.

## The 10-week arc

**Week 1 - Orientation.** You are here. This week you choose your problem, identify who is affected by it, and set yourself up for the sprint work ahead.

**Weeks 2-9 - Four two-week sprints.** Each sprint builds your ability to frame and articulate your problem. You will use AI as a thinking partner, talk to real stakeholders in your organization, and produce a visible artifact each sprint. The work compounds: what you produce in Sprint 1 informs Sprint 2, and so on.

**Week 10 - Capstone.** You present your fully framed problem to peers and stakeholders. The capstone is not a test of recall. It is evidence of what you can now do.

## What "real problem" means

Your problem must come from your actual workplace. It should involve real people who are affected by it, and real consequences if it goes unaddressed. It does not need to be fully formed right now -- clarity is the outcome of the course, not the prerequisite.

What it cannot be: a hypothetical, a case study, or a problem borrowed from someone else's organization. You need access to the people affected. You will be talking to them.

## Real stakeholders, not simulated ones

Every sprint includes a stakeholder touchpoint. That means an actual conversation with someone in your organization -- a colleague, a manager, a customer, a direct report, whoever is genuinely affected by your problem. You already know these people. The course mechanics are designed to give you a reason to talk to them and a structure for making that conversation useful.

## Time commitment

Plan for 3-5 hours per week. That includes reading, AI-assisted work, stakeholder conversations, and submitting your artifact. The activities are sequenced so that each one delivers value the same week you do it -- not setup for something that pays off later.

If a week runs long, something in the design is not working. Let your instructor know.

## How AI fits in

You will use AI throughout this course as a thinking partner, not as a shortcut. The goal is a working relationship where you bring what AI cannot -- your knowledge of your organization, your judgment about what matters, your accountability for the outcome -- and AI contributes what it does well. That partnership is what the course is teaching. It is also what makes the work yours.
