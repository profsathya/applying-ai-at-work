## Assumption Audit with AI

Take the problem statement you wrote last week and use it as input for an AI tool. Your job in this assignment is not to improve the statement - it is to interrogate it. AI is good at generating a long list of possibilities quickly. You are good at knowing which ones are actually true in your context. That combination is what this assignment is about.

## What to do

**Step 1: Run the prompt.**

Paste your problem statement into an AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot - whichever you have access to) and use a prompt like this:

> "Here is a problem statement I wrote about a situation at work. List every assumption embedded in this statement - about the people involved, the causes, the stakes, what a solution would look like, and anything else you notice. Be thorough."

**Step 2: Read the list critically.**

The AI will generate more assumptions than are actually present. That is fine. Your job is to read the list as someone who knows the actual situation. Some items will be accurate. Some will be generic or wrong. Some may name something real that you had not consciously thought about.

**Step 3: Decide what to keep and what to reject.**

Go through the list and classify each assumption. You do not need to keep the AI's exact wording - restate in your own terms where useful.

**Step 4: Identify one to test.**

Pick one assumption that you kept - one that matters for how you framed the problem - and identify a specific person you could talk to in order to find out whether it is true.

## What to submit

Write a short response covering these four things:

1. **The assumptions AI surfaced.** List them. Brief phrases are fine - you do not need full sentences for each one.
2. **Which ones genuinely surprised you.** Name at least one, and say briefly why it landed differently than you expected.
3. **Which ones you rejected, and why.** Name at least two you pushed back on. For each, say what was wrong or too generic about it given your actual context.
4. **One assumption you want to test.** State the assumption, name the specific person you plan to talk to, and say what you would ask them.

Total length: 300 to 500 words. Use the four headings above to organize your response.

## What this is not

This is not a revision of your problem statement. Do not rewrite the statement here. The original stands as your baseline. This assignment is about examining what that statement takes for granted.

It is also not a test of the AI tool. Whether the list was impressive or mediocre is beside the point. What matters is what you brought to it: your judgment about which items reflect reality and which do not.

## Evaluation

Your response will be evaluated on whether your own judgment is visible. A list of AI-generated assumptions with no pushback earns partial credit. What earns full credit is evidence that you filtered the AI's output through your knowledge of the actual situation - keeping what is accurate, rejecting what is not, and naming something specific enough to act on.
