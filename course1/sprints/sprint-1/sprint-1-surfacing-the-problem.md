# Sprint 1: Surfacing the Problem

You have a problem. Over the next two weeks, you will sharpen it.

Most workplace problems start as a vague sense that something is off: a process that feels broken, a decision that keeps getting made wrong, a gap between what people say and what actually happens. That vague concern is a starting point, not a destination. This sprint asks you to move from "something is wrong here" to a specific, articulable problem statement - one that names what is at stake, who is affected, and why it matters.

The sprint ends with a real conversation. Not a survey, not a form - an actual exchange with someone in your organization who has a stake in the problem you are framing. What you learn from that conversation will reshape what you thought you knew.
