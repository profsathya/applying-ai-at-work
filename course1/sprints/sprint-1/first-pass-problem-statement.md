## First-Pass Problem Statement

Write one paragraph describing the problem you chose to work on. Do this without AI assistance.

Your paragraph should cover four things:

1. **The problem.** What is happening, or not happening, that should be different?
2. **Who is affected.** Which specific people or groups feel the consequences?
3. **Why it matters now.** What makes this urgent or consequential at this point in time?
4. **A better state.** What would be different if the problem were addressed?

Aim for four to six sentences. Concrete and specific beats polished and general. Write in the language you would use with a colleague, not in report-speak.

Do not use AI to draft, revise, or improve this paragraph. That is not a technicality - the value of this assignment comes from capturing what you actually think right now, before any outside input shapes it. You will return to this draft at the end of the course and compare it to where you end up. That comparison is one of the most useful things you will produce this semester.

## Submission

Paste your paragraph directly into the text box. At the end, add a single line confirming you wrote the draft without AI assistance. For example: "Confirmed: written without AI."

## Why this assignment exists

Your thinking about this problem will change. By the end of the course, you will likely be able to identify who is actually affected (as opposed to who you assume is affected), articulate the stakes more precisely, and describe a desired state that is achievable rather than aspirational. None of that is visible unless you have a baseline to compare against. This paragraph is that baseline.

It will almost certainly be incomplete or wrong in ways you cannot yet see. That is expected and fine.
