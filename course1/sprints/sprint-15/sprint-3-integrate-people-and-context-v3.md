# Sprint 3: Integrate People and Context (V3)

In this sprint, you move the problem outside your own head. You map stakeholders, prepare listening questions, conduct real validation, and revise your frame based on what a real person helps you see.

You will keep using the same real current problem throughout the course. Remember that a variety of problems are acceptable, many will fall under workplace problems, job search, role transition, volunteer role, community work, caregiving or family system, campus work, professional portfolio, small business, or another real setting where actual people are affected.

By the end of this sprint, you should be able to:

1. Identify stakeholders and their relationship to the problem.
2. Design validation questions for stakeholders that produce real evidence.
3. Revise your Problem Frame using human input and context.

## Sprint Rhythm

1. Read the Introduction and pass the concept check.
2. Build your stakeholder map and choose who you will talk to first.
3. Test and widen it with AI.
4. Prepare, hold, and document your stakeholder conversation.
5. Reflect and revise your Problem Frame.

## Portfolio Thread

Carry forward the work from this sprint into your living course portfolio. Keep the artifacts, AI prompts, pushback notes, stakeholder evidence, and reflections that show how your thinking changed.

## Completion Criteria

You are done when your Problem Frame reflects at least one real stakeholder conversation and names what still needs validation.
