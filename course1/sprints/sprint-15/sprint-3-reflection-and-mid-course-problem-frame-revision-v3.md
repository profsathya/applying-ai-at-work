# Sprint 3 Reflection and Mid-Course Problem Frame Revision

## Purpose

Pause at mid-course to name what stakeholder evidence changed and how your Problem Frame should adapt.

## Task Instructions

1. Review your stakeholder map, your Stakeholder Validation Report, and your revised Problem Frame.
2. Compare what your Dojo Lab work anticipated with what a real person actually helped you see.
3. Write an initial reflection and Problem Frame revision in the activity.
4. Generate AI follow-up questions using the built-in AI activity, and use them to identify what is still vague, generic, or unsupported.
5. Revise your reflection so the stakeholder evidence and the Problem Frame change are clear.
6. Use the deeper AI guidance button if your answer smooths over tension or conflict.
7. Save the refined response in the activity.

This activity runs on the built-in AI activity, not your own AI chat.

## Deliverable

Your refined reflection should answer the four questions below. Half a page to a page across all four is the right size.

- What a real person helped you see that AI did not.
- What part of your frame changed, and why.
- What tension or conflict you are carrying forward.
- What you will validate next, and what that changes for the rest of the course.

## Submission Format

Download the JSON response file from the activity and upload it to Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your final response should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

Save this as the mid-course version of your Problem Frame.
