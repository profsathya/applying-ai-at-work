# Which assumptions

This is the write-up of the walk. You have looked closely at your assumptions, rated how risky each is to your problem, and had AI push on all of it. Put it here so that your instructor can see the reasoning and so that you have it to come back to in week 2, when you bring your frame up to date.

Paste your assumption tables, every one, as they stand after the Dojo Lab. If the Dojo Lab changed a rating or a reason, paste the changed version; the tables are yours and they are supposed to move. Then one line underneath: what the Dojo Lab pushed on and what you changed or kept, with the reason. If you did not use the Dojo Lab, say so and why.

> **Example, the line underneath.** It pushed on "one person's habit, nothing here," and I changed that row: one person's habit can still expose a process that fails whenever accounts move. It pushed on "one day is fast enough to matter" as low risk, and I kept the rating with a better reason.
