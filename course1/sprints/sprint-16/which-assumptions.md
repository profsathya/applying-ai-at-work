# Your updated assumptions

This is where your week 1 work gets read. Two parts.

## Part 1: Your updated tables

Paste your **Dig into your assumptions** tables from your **Sprint 2 working book**, every one, as they stand after the Dojo Lab. If the Dojo Lab changed a rating or a reason, paste the changed version; the tables are yours and they are supposed to move.

## Part 2: What the Dojo changed

In your own words: what the Dojo pushed on, what you changed, and what you kept and why. A few sentences is plenty. If you did not use the Dojo Lab, say so and why.

> **Example.** It pushed on "one person's habit, nothing here," and I changed that row: one person's habit can still expose a process that fails whenever accounts move. It pushed on "one day is fast enough to matter" as low risk, and I kept the rating with a better reason, that the length of time does not change whether this is worth pursuing.
