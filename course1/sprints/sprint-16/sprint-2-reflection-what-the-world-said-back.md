This sprint you rated the risk in your own assumptions and looked at what already exists. What changed and what you kept is already in your two write-ups, so this is about how you worked, in three parts. Where was it hardest to write "I don't know" instead of a guess, and what did you do? What did you notice about how you make assumptions, now that you have walked a whole set of them? And what is the one thing in your frame you are least sure how to find out?
