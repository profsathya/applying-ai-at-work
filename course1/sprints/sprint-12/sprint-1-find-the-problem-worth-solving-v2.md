# Sprint 1: Find the Problem Worth Solving (V2)

Start with situations from your own life, work, or community. You do not need to arrive with a chosen problem. In this first half, you build a list, find the gaps underneath it, and choose one problem to carry into framing.

You will practice three things:

- Describe what actually happens so you can see a gap worth investigating.
- Recognize when a request already contains a solution, then identify the goal and possible problems underneath it.
- Compare candidate problems and explain a choice using what you know, with uncertainty still visible.

## Your route

Read the Introduction, then work through Start your list and get underneath it, Things you stopped noticing, and Things somebody handed you. These three own-your-progress activities build one table. They are encouraged, carry zero points, and do not block the next item if you skip them.

Complete the six-question Concept check, then use Test and commit to turn your table into candidate problems, run the four checks, and explain your choice. The self-check carries 5 completion points reviewed in Canvas. Test and commit is one 35-point submission.

## What you carry forward

Keep your table, candidate problems, check answers, uncertainty marks, and commitment reasons. If no candidate survives, record what you tried and the next place you will look. This first half ends with your choice; the second half will use the problem you choose to develop a first frame.
