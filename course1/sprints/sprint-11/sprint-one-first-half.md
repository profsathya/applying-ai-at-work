This is the first half of Sprint One. You will turn things you notice in your own life into candidate problems, examine which ones are workable, and choose one with reasons you can defend.

Start with your own observations. Use AI after you have written and checked your candidates, then decide which challenges fit the evidence you actually have. Your choice will carry forward into framing the problem, talking with real people who have a stake in it, and identifying what you still need to learn.

Keep one working document as you go. Save your initial list, candidate gaps, uncertainty marks, check answers, AI challenges and responses, and final choice with a runner-up. You will submit the Candidate List first and Test and Commit after the four checks. The concept check helps you examine the ideas between those submissions.

By the end of this half, you should have a defensible starting problem. You do not need to have proved every claim or completed stakeholder inquiry yet.
