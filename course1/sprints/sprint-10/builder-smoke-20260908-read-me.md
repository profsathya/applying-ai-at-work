# Builder Smoke Test: Read Me

Use this optional technical review module to inspect a short page, a text-entry assignment, and a three-question quiz. Allow about three minutes if you choose to try the activities. You do not need to complete this test for your course work.

For the activities, use these three definitions:

- A **goal** is what you want to achieve.
- A **problem** is an obstacle that makes your goal harder to reach.
- A **stakeholder** is a person affected by the work or its outcome.

Your assignment asks for one sentence about a real work task. Your quiz checks the definitions above. Keep names, customer information, and other sensitive details out of any response.

Open the assignment next to inspect its text-entry instructions, then open the quiz to review its three questions.

You may inspect all three items without submitting a response.
