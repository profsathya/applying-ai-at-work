# Set up your AI Dojo

Set up one AI tool as a coach you can return to throughout the course. Your **Dojo** is a place to examine your thinking: you supply observations, try an answer, and make decisions while the coach asks questions and helps you test your reasoning.

This setup has no submission. **Work without AI in the first half of Sprint 1**, as its activity directions require. Use the setup check below to test your coach without doing that work.

## Choose your tool

Choose one tool you already use and sign in on the web. Name your project or Gem **CIS 501 AI Dojo**, then find its instructions field:

- **ChatGPT:** choose **New project** in the sidebar. Open the project's three-dot menu, then **Project settings** to add instructions. [ChatGPT project help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt).
- **Claude:** open **Projects**, choose **New Project**, then **Set project instructions**. [Claude project help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9519177-how-can-i-create-and-manage-projects).
- **Gemini:** open **Gems**, then **New Gem**, and use the **Instructions** field. If Gems is not visible, check **Settings and help**. Save the Gem before leaving. [Gemini Gem help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780?hl=en).

Use a model available in your account. If your account does not offer projects or Gems, use a regular chat and paste the instructions and method at the start of each new conversation.

## Give your coach the method

Open the [shared Dojo Core](https://profsathya.github.io/Common-Curriculum/common/dojo/dojo-core.txt). This is the reusable coaching method; it does not contain this course's activities or policies.

Open the [Dojo and Course Context document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1V8L_oJVFwYVtaJmJoVBj4QSSGQXERdjRfhm4NlryAjE/edit). Its **Dojo** tab contains the shared method; its **Course** tab contains released course material with source links and a generation date. The initial course snapshot is dated September 16, 2026. Automatic refresh is not enabled yet, so check current activity directions in Canvas.

Add that document as a source: in ChatGPT, use **Add source** in your project and paste the Drive link; in Claude, add it from **Google Drive** to project knowledge; in Gemini, select it from **Drive** under the Gem's **Knowledge**. Availability depends on your account. If the tool cannot access both tabs, supply dated copies of their text and replace those copies when the document changes.

Copy the following instruction into your project's or Gem's instructions field and save it. In a regular chat, send it as your first message.

> Act as my Dojo coach for CIS 501: Reframing Problems with AI. Use the shared Dojo Core at https://profsathya.github.io/Common-Curriculum/common/dojo/dojo-core.txt to guide how you coach me.
>
> At the start of each new chat, try to read the current method. Say which source you actually accessed. If you cannot retrieve it, tell me before coaching and ask me to supply its text. If I provide a saved copy, identify it as a dated snapshot, not a live source.
>
> I supply my thinking and make my own decisions. Ask one focused question at a time and help me examine evidence and assumptions. Do not invent experiences, stakeholder responses, or evidence for me.
>
> The method's references to document tabs apply only when those tabs are actually available. Ask me for the relevant course directions when you do not have them. Answer factual course questions from the supplied material and identify the source; do not invent policies, deadlines, or requirements. Respect directions to work without AI.
>
> Use the attached Dojo and Course Context document for course context. At the start of a new chat, try to read its Course tab and report the release and generation date you accessed. If it is unavailable or conflicts with current Canvas directions I supply, flag that limitation before answering.

**If the link cannot be read:** copy the complete Core text from the link, or save it as a text file. Add it to ChatGPT's project sources, Claude's project knowledge, or the Gem's **Knowledge → Add files**. Include the date you obtained it in the file name or beside the pasted text. For a regular chat, paste the complete text into that conversation.

A saved copy does not update automatically. Replace it from the shared link when the method changes, remove the older copy, and start a new chat. A new chat alone does not prove that a linked source was read.

## Check that it coaches you

Start a chat inside your project or with your saved Gem. Ask which method it accessed and how it will handle a request to make a decision for you. Compare its explanation with the Core's opening rule. A version number or a claim that it read the file is not enough by itself.

Then give it one real, non-sensitive sentence about what helps you learn and one thing you are unsure about when learning with AI. Ask it to help you examine that uncertainty. Answer its follow-up in your own words.

Check the exchange:

- Does it work with what you actually said and ask a focused follow-up?
- Does it ask for your rough judgment before choosing for you?
- Does it question an unsupported assumption when one arises, rather than supplying invented evidence?

If it writes a finished answer for you, remind it to follow the Core and ask for your thinking first. If it cannot access the method, use the saved-copy route above and repeat the check. You do not need to submit or export this conversation.

## Return for later activities

When an activity invites AI use, open this same project or Gem and supply your own notes and the question you want to examine. Confirm that it can access the relevant course directions; supply them yourself when it cannot. Check factual answers against the source in Canvas; ask your instructor when a requirement remains unclear.

Keep workplace details appropriate for the tool you use. Your own observations and contact with real stakeholders remain the evidence for your work.
