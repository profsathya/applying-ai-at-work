# Espresso Diagnosis Exchange

Post one espresso shot log and ask for a practical diagnosis. Keep the post concise enough that a busy colleague could read it and offer a useful next step.

## Your post

Include:

- Dose in grams.
- Yield in grams.
- Time and when you started timing.
- Grind setting or adjustment direction if your grinder settings are not numeric.
- Puck prep notes, including distribution and tamp.
- What you saw during extraction.
- Tasting notes using at least three categories from the tasting page.
- One adjustment you are considering for the next shot.

End with one specific question for the group. For example:

- "Would you adjust grind or yield first?"
- "Does this sound more like channeling or a grind-size problem?"
- "What tasting note would make you stop the shot earlier?"

## Reply to one peer

Reply to one peer with:

- One diagnosis grounded in their log.
- One suggested next adjustment.
- One reason you would make that adjustment before changing anything else.

Do not tell them to change everything at once. The goal is to help them run a cleaner next test.

## Evaluation

Criterion | Points
---|---:
Post includes a complete shot log with dose, yield, timing, puck prep, flow, and tasting notes | 4
Post asks a specific diagnosis question | 2
Reply gives a practical, evidence-based adjustment | 3
Reply explains why that adjustment should come before other changes | 1

**Total: 10 points**
