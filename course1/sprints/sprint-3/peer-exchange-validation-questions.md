# Peer Exchange: Validation Questions

## Purpose

Use peers to improve the questions you will ask a real stakeholder. The best questions help you listen, not pitch.

## Initial Post

Post:

1. Three validation questions you plan to ask.
2. The stakeholder role you hope to ask.
3. What you need to learn from the conversation.

## Replies

Reply to at least two peers. In each reply, address:

1. Which question sounds like listening.
2. Which question sounds like pitching.
3. What question is missing.

## Discussion Standard

Be concrete and respectful. Help the other person see what is clear, what still sounds like an assumption, and what would make the work stronger. Do not solve their problem for them.
