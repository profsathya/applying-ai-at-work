# Stakeholder Map Draft

## Purpose

Create a working stakeholder map that shows who is connected to the problem and what you still need to learn from them.

## Task Instructions

1. Use your Dojo preparation, current problem frame, and your own context knowledge.
2. Include stakeholders across affected people, influencers, and people with authority or constraints.
3. Do not overstate what you know. Mark evidence status clearly.
4. Choose the next question you need to ask for each stakeholder role.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- Stakeholder role.
- Relationship to the problem.
- Impact level.
- Influence or authority level.
- What they need from a better state.
- What constraint or risk they may see.
- Evidence source: known, inferred, or validated.
- Next question to ask.

## Submission Format

Text entry in Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your submission should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

This draft becomes the basis for your validation conversation and final stakeholder report.
