# Dojo Lab: Stakeholder Lens Preparation

## Purpose

Prepare for real stakeholder validation by using AI to anticipate perspectives, assumptions, and listening questions.

## Pre-Dojo Pause

Before using AI, list the stakeholders you already believe are affected by the problem. Mark what you know and what you are guessing.

## Dojo Prompt

Use this prompt with the Dojo:

```text
I am preparing to validate this problem with real people:
[paste problem statement]

Act as Stakeholder Lens. Help me identify:
1. People directly affected.
2. People who influence whether the problem can be addressed.
3. People with authority or constraints.
4. People who may disagree with my framing.
5. What each stakeholder might need, fear, or value.

Then act as Skeptic:
- Which stakeholders am I overlooking?
- Which needs am I assuming rather than knowing?
- Which validation questions would help me listen rather than pitch?
```

## Judgment Step

Review the Dojo output and mark each stakeholder need as known, inferred, or not yet supported. Do not treat the Dojo as stakeholder evidence.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- Stakeholder groups.
- Likely needs and constraints.
- Assumptions that require real conversation.
- Five validation questions.
- One stakeholder you will contact first and why.

## Portfolio Capture

Save this preparation with your stakeholder map. It should help you prepare for validation, not replace it.
