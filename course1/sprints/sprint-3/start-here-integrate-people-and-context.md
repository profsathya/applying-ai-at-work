# Start Here: Integrate People And Context

## Why This Sprint Matters

A problem frame becomes stronger when it meets real people and real constraints. This sprint asks you to map stakeholders, prepare listening questions, and conduct validation that can confirm, challenge, or complicate your frame.

You will use AI as a thinking partner, but you remain responsible for the goal, standards, choices, and final meaning of the work. Start with your own thinking, use AI to expand and test it, then decide what to keep, reject, revise, or verify.

## What You Will Do

1. Identify affected, influential, and authority stakeholders.
2. Use the Dojo to prepare for listening without replacing real contact.
3. Draft a stakeholder map with honest evidence status.
4. Conduct at least one real validation conversation.
5. Revise your problem statement and Goal Plan based on what you learn.

## Time Guidance

Plan for focused work across two weeks. Short concept reading, Dojo practice, peer exchange, and a final artifact should all serve the same real problem rather than becoming separate assignments.

## Working Standard

Specific and honest work is better than polished generic work. Show what you tried, what changed, what evidence you have, and what remains uncertain.

## Carry Forward

Keep stakeholder map versions, validation questions, notes from the real conversation, revisions, and remaining validation needs.
