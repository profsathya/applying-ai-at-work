# Stakeholder Validation Report

## Purpose

Conduct at least one real validation conversation and revise the problem based on what you learn.

## Task Instructions

1. Contact a real person connected to your chosen context. If you cannot access a workplace stakeholder, contact a real mentor, peer, advisor, community member, target user, volunteer coordinator, customer, family system participant, or professional contact.
2. Ask questions that test the problem frame rather than pitch a solution.
3. Take notes on what the person said in their own terms. Do not include confidential details.
4. Revise your problem statement and Goal Plan based on evidence.

## Deliverable

Submit a text entry with:

- Current problem statement before validation.
- Stakeholder contacted, described by role and relationship.
- Conversation method and date. Do not include confidential details.
- What the stakeholder said in their own terms.
- Where the stakeholder confirmed the frame.
- Where the stakeholder challenged, complicated, or contradicted the frame.
- Revised problem statement.
- Stakeholder needs and constraints matrix.
- Mid-course Goal Plan revision.
- What still needs validation.

## Submission Format

Text entry in Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your submission should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

This report is one of the core evidence sources for your final readiness report.
