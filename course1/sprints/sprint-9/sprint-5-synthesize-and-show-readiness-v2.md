# Sprint 5: Synthesize And Show Readiness (V2)

In this sprint, you build one argument out of everything you have made since Sprint 1. You turn your artifacts into a claim a reader can follow, find the gaps in it before a reader does, and say what your readiness rests on.

This sprint is part of Reframing Problems with AI. You will keep using the same real current problem throughout the course. A workplace problem is ideal, but the problem can also come from a job search, role transition, volunteer role, community work, caregiving or family system, campus work, professional portfolio, small business, or another real setting where actual people are affected.

By the end of this sprint, you should be able to:

1. Turn course artifacts into a coherent argument about the problem and your readiness.
2. Identify gaps in evidence, logic, and stakeholder grounding before final submission.
3. Submit a final integrated problem document that a reader can follow.

## Sprint Rhythm

1. Read the Introduction and orient to the readiness standard.
2. Draft the integrated problem document.
3. Audit it for gaps in evidence, logic, and grounding.
4. Submit the final document and close the loop on your Goal Plan.

## Portfolio Thread

This sprint consumes the portfolio rather than adding to it. Bring your problem frame from Sprint 1, your prompting record from Sprint 2, your stakeholder evidence from Sprint 3, and your learning evidence from Sprint 4.

## Completion Criteria

You are done when a reader who has never seen your work can follow your argument, see what it rests on, and tell what you are ready to do next.
