# Sprint 5 Reflection

This reflection is not about your assignments. It is about you, and how the course landed for you. In Sprint 1 you were told that by Sprint 5 you would read all four sprint reflections back. This is that moment.

## How to do it

1. Read back your four earlier sprint reflections first.
2. Then answer the three questions below in your own words in the Canvas text box. Number your answers 1 to 3.

## The three questions

1. **What is your biggest takeaway from the course, and what was hardest about pulling it all together?** *(2-4 sentences)*
2. **Is there anything you learned or noticed that none of the activities asked about?** It could be about your problem, about working with AI, or about yourself. *(2-4 sentences, or "nothing new")*
3. **How did the pace and workload land for you over the course?** *(1-2 sentences)*

## What a strong reflection looks like

- You answer all three questions, honestly and specifically.
- You wrote it yourself, without AI. We want your unedited thinking and personal reflection.
