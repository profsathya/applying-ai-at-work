# Concept: From Artifacts To Argument

## Synthesis Is Selection

A final report is not every assignment pasted together. Synthesis means choosing the evidence that supports a clear argument: this is the problem, this is why it matters, this is what I learned, this is what remains uncertain, and this is the next responsible move.

## Readiness Is A Judgment

Readiness does not mean certainty. It means you can make a responsible claim about whether the problem is ready to carry forward, ready with conditions, or not ready yet. Each option can be strong if the reasoning is honest and evidence-based.

## Show The Change Over Time

The final report should make your learning visible. Name what changed from Week 1 to Week 10: the problem frame, stakeholder understanding, AI direction, learning priorities, scope, or evidence standard.

## Name The Human Contribution

AI may have helped generate options, organize evidence, and challenge assumptions. You contributed context, relationships, restraint, accountability, ethical judgment, and final meaning. The report should make that split specific to your problem.

## Practice This In Your Work

- Choose the evidence your reader needs, not everything you produced.
- Connect stakeholder evidence to the final problem frame.
- Explain what AI helped with and where you pushed back.
- Make a readiness claim with conditions if needed.
- End with the next move.

## Check Your Understanding

Before you move to the next activity, write one sentence in your notes that names the decision you need to make next. If the sentence could apply to anyone, add more of your own context.
