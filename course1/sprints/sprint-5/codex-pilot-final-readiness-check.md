# Codex Pilot: Final Readiness Check

Use this short submission to document your final revision priorities before the capstone is submitted. You are not being asked to summarize the whole project. You are being asked to show that you can inspect your own work and make a professional decision about what still needs attention.

## What to submit

Type a brief response directly into the text entry box. Address the three prompts below.

1. **What is the strongest part of your current capstone draft?** Name the part of the work that is most ready for a stakeholder or outside reader. Be specific.
2. **What is the most important readiness gap?** Identify one gap that could weaken the final submission if left unresolved. Point to evidence from your current draft, outline, or notes.
3. **What will you revise before submission?** Name one or two concrete actions you will take. Explain why those actions will make the capstone clearer, more credible, or more useful to the people affected by the problem.

Your response should be concise. A few focused paragraphs are enough.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Readiness gaps are identified with specific evidence from the current capstone draft | 5 |
| Revision priorities are concrete, realistic, and tied to stakeholder clarity | 5 |

**Total: 10 points**

A strong response shows that you know where the capstone is already working and where your final revision effort will have the highest value.
