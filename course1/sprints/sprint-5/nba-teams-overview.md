# NBA Teams Overview

The NBA has 30 teams split between the Eastern Conference and the Western Conference. Each conference has three divisions with five teams each. This overview follows the official NBA teams list and gives a simple starting point for recognizing where each team fits.

## Eastern Conference

### Atlantic Division

  * **Boston Celtics:** The Boston Celtics play in Boston and are one of the league’s longest-running franchises.
  * **Brooklyn Nets:** The Brooklyn Nets play in New York City and represent the Brooklyn borough.
  * **New York Knicks:** The New York Knicks play in Manhattan and are one of New York’s major basketball teams.
  * **Philadelphia 76ers:** The Philadelphia 76ers play in Philadelphia and are often called the Sixers.
  * **Toronto Raptors:** The Toronto Raptors play in Toronto and are the NBA’s only team based in Canada.



### Central Division

  * **Chicago Bulls:** The Chicago Bulls play in Chicago and are known for a strong basketball history.
  * **Cleveland Cavaliers:** The Cleveland Cavaliers play in Cleveland and are often called the Cavs.
  * **Detroit Pistons:** The Detroit Pistons play in Detroit and represent Michigan in the NBA.
  * **Indiana Pacers:** The Indiana Pacers play in Indianapolis and represent Indiana’s basketball tradition.
  * **Milwaukee Bucks:** The Milwaukee Bucks play in Milwaukee and represent Wisconsin in the NBA.



### Southeast Division

  * **Atlanta Hawks:** The Atlanta Hawks play in Atlanta and represent Georgia in the NBA.
  * **Charlotte Hornets:** The Charlotte Hornets play in Charlotte and represent North Carolina in the NBA.
  * **Miami Heat:** The Miami Heat play in Miami and represent South Florida in the NBA.
  * **Orlando Magic:** The Orlando Magic play in Orlando and are one of Florida’s two NBA teams.
  * **Washington Wizards:** The Washington Wizards play in Washington, D.C. and represent the nation’s capital.



## Western Conference

### Northwest Division

  * **Denver Nuggets:** The Denver Nuggets play in Denver and represent Colorado in the NBA.
  * **Minnesota Timberwolves:** The Minnesota Timberwolves play in Minneapolis and represent Minnesota in the NBA.
  * **Oklahoma City Thunder:** The Oklahoma City Thunder play in Oklahoma City and represent Oklahoma in the NBA.
  * **Portland Trail Blazers:** The Portland Trail Blazers play in Portland and represent Oregon in the NBA.
  * **Utah Jazz:** The Utah Jazz play in Salt Lake City and represent Utah in the NBA.



### Pacific Division

  * **Golden State Warriors:** The Golden State Warriors play in San Francisco and represent the Bay Area.
  * **LA Clippers:** The LA Clippers play in Los Angeles and share the city with another NBA team.
  * **Los Angeles Lakers:** The Los Angeles Lakers play in Los Angeles and are one of the league’s most recognized teams.
  * **Phoenix Suns:** The Phoenix Suns play in Phoenix and represent Arizona in the NBA.
  * **Sacramento Kings:** The Sacramento Kings play in Sacramento and represent California’s capital city.



### Southwest Division

  * **Dallas Mavericks:** The Dallas Mavericks play in Dallas and are often called the Mavs.
  * **Houston Rockets:** The Houston Rockets play in Houston and represent one of Texas’s largest cities.
  * **Memphis Grizzlies:** The Memphis Grizzlies play in Memphis and represent Tennessee in the NBA.
  * **New Orleans Pelicans:** The New Orleans Pelicans play in New Orleans and represent Louisiana in the NBA.
  * **San Antonio Spurs:** The San Antonio Spurs play in San Antonio and represent South Texas in the NBA.
