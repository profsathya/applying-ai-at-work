# Codex Pilot: Capstone Readiness Sprint

This supplemental sprint gives you a focused readiness check before you submit your final capstone work. It is intentionally small. The goal is not to add a new project, but to help you notice what still needs attention in the work you have already built.

Use this sprint to step back from the final artifact and look at it the way a stakeholder would. Your capstone should make the problem, the evidence, the judgment calls, and the final recommendation clear to someone who was not present for the full course.

## What you will do

- Review the capstone expectations against your current draft or outline.
- Identify the one or two gaps most likely to weaken the final submission.
- Decide what you will revise before the capstone is submitted.

## What readiness means

Readiness does not mean the work is perfect. It means the final submission is coherent, grounded in real workplace evidence, and clear about what you contributed that AI could not supply on its own.

By the end of this short sprint, you should know exactly what you will leave as is, what you will revise, and why those choices fit your professional context.
