# Favorite NBA Team Discussion

Choose one NBA team you enjoy watching, follow casually, or want to learn more about.

## Your Post

Write a short discussion post that addresses all three prompts:

  1. Name your favorite NBA team.
  2. Explain why you chose that team. You might discuss a player, coach, city connection, playing style, team history, or a memorable game.
  3. Share one thing you learned about that team. This can be about the roster, record, history, style of play, fan culture, or an important moment in the team’s season.



Use specific details so other participants can understand what stands out to you about the team.

## Peer Reply

Reply to one peer. In your response, do at least one of the following:

  * Ask a genuine question about their team or reason for choosing it.
  * Make a connection to something you know about that team.
  * Share one thing their post helped you notice or understand.



Avoid generic replies. A useful response should show that you read the peer’s post closely.
