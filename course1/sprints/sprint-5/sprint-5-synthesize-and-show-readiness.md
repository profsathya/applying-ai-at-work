# Sprint 5: Synthesize And Show Readiness

In this sprint, you turn prior work into a coherent readiness argument. Synthesis is not pasting assignments together. It is choosing evidence, naming gaps, and making a defensible judgment about what is ready to carry forward.

This sprint is part of Reframing Problems with AI. You will keep using the same real current problem throughout the course. A workplace problem is ideal, but the problem can also come from a job search, role transition, volunteer role, community work, caregiving or family system, campus work, professional portfolio, small business, or another real setting where actual people are affected.

By the end of this sprint, you should be able to:

1. Select and connect evidence from the full course portfolio.
2. Explain how the problem changed from Week 1 to Week 10.
3. Make a readiness judgment that is honest and evidence-based.
4. Name what you contributed that AI alone could not.

## Sprint Rhythm

1. Audit your portfolio for evidence, repetition, gaps, and human judgment.
2. Read the concept page on turning artifacts into an argument.
3. Draft an integrated problem document and get peer review.
4. Revise the final readiness report for an outside reader.
5. Complete the closing reflection and final Goal Plan check.

## Portfolio Thread

Carry forward the work from this sprint into your living course portfolio. Keep the artifacts, AI prompts, pushback notes, stakeholder evidence, and reflections that show how your thinking changed.

## Completion Criteria

You are done when an outside reader can understand the problem, why it matters, what evidence supports it, what remains uncertain, and what you are ready to do next.
