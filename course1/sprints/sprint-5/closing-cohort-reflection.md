# Closing Cohort Reflection

You came into this course with a workplace problem and a general sense that AI could be useful somehow. You are leaving with a validated problem frame, a stakeholder map, tested assumptions, and a documented record of the judgment you exercised along the way. That is a real change. This discussion makes it visible -- to you and to the people who worked alongside you.

## Your post

Write two things.

1. **One thing you can now do with a workplace problem that you could not do in Week 1.** Be specific. Not "I can use AI better" -- that is too vague to be useful to you or to anyone reading it. Something like: "I can scope a messy organizational problem to the point where I can tell a stakeholder exactly what we are and are not trying to solve." Or: "I can run a stakeholder conversation that actually tests my assumptions instead of confirming them." Name the capability. Ground it in something from your work this semester.

2. **One thing you want to carry into Course 2.** This does not have to be a skill. It can be a question that is still open, a habit you want to push further, or something from your capstone work you want to keep building on. What are you not done with yet?

There is no required length. A short, specific post is better than a long, general one.

## Replies

Reply to at least two peers. Focus your replies on posts where something surprised you: a growth they named that you would not have predicted, or a direction they are heading that you want to know more about. Do not write generic encouragement. Ask a question, make a connection to your own experience, or say specifically what caught your attention and why.
