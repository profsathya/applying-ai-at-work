# Codex Agent Practice Peer Exchange

Share the small Codex request you drafted, then compare review strategies with peers. The purpose is to make the workflow more concrete: what you ask for, what boundaries you set, and what you inspect before anything reaches Canvas.

## Your post

Write a brief post with three parts.

1. **Your request.** Paste or summarize the plain-English request you drafted.
2. **Your most important guardrail.** Name the one instruction that most reduces risk, such as stopping before Canvas push, avoiding file uploads, or limiting edits to a folder.
3. **Your review focus.** Name one thing you would look for in the generated Markdown before approving any next step.

Keep the post practical. You are not explaining Codex in general. You are showing how you would use it for one small course-building task.

## Replies

Reply to at least two peers. In each reply, do one useful thing:

- Identify a constraint that could make their request clearer.
- Name a review check they might add.
- Point out where their request is already specific enough to reduce risk.

Avoid generic encouragement. The most useful replies help someone make the next request clearer and safer.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Initial post includes a concrete request, guardrail, and review focus | 6 |
| Two replies give practical feedback on request clarity or review steps | 4 |

**Total: 10 points**
