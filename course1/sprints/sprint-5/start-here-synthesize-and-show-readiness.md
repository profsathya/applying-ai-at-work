# Start Here: Synthesize And Show Readiness

## Why This Sprint Matters

You now have a goal plan, problem frame, AI exploration log, stakeholder evidence, and learning journey. This sprint asks you to synthesize that work into one coherent readiness report for an outside reader.

You will use AI as a thinking partner, but you remain responsible for the goal, standards, choices, and final meaning of the work. Start with your own thinking, use AI to expand and test it, then decide what to keep, reject, revise, or verify.

## What You Will Do

1. Audit your portfolio for useful evidence and gaps.
2. Use the Dojo to organize the final argument without letting it write the judgment for you.
3. Draft the integrated problem document.
4. Use peer review to check whether the argument is clear.
5. Submit the final readiness report and closing reflection.

## Two-Week Sprint Map

This sprint spans two course weeks. Use these labels for pacing; Canvas keeps the work in one module.

- Sprint Week 1: Start here, read the concept page, use the Dojo to audit gaps, and draft the integrated problem document.
- Sprint Week 2: Complete the AI peer review, submit the final readiness report, refine the AI-guided closing reflection, and complete the readiness self-check.

## Time Guidance

Plan for focused work across two weeks. Short concept reading, Dojo practice, peer exchange, and a final artifact should all serve the same real problem rather than becoming separate assignments.

## Working Standard

Specific and honest work is better than polished generic work. Show what you tried, what changed, what evidence you have, and what remains uncertain.

## Carry Forward

Carry forward only the evidence that helps the reader understand the problem, the revision path, the stakeholder reality, the learning gap, and your readiness judgment.
