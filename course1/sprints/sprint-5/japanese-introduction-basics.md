# Japanese Introduction Basics

In this module, you will write and say a short self-introduction in Japanese. You will use hiragana and katakana where you can, and you will include romaji pronunciation beside your Japanese text so classmates can read it more easily.

The goal is a clear, respectful introduction. It does not need to be long.

## A simple introduction pattern

Use this pattern as your starting point:
    
    
    こんにちは。
    Konnichiwa.
    
    わたしは [name] です。
    Watashi wa [name] desu.
    
    よろしくおねがいします。
    Yoroshiku onegai shimasu.
    

Meaning:

  * `こんにちは` means hello.
  * `わたしは` means I am. The character `は` is pronounced `wa` when it marks the topic.
  * `[name] です` means I am \[name\].
  * `よろしくおねがいします` is a polite closing often used when meeting someone. A natural English meaning is please treat me well, or nice to meet you.



## Add your name in katakana

Names from languages other than Japanese are usually written in katakana. Katakana represents sound, not exact English spelling. Choose the closest sound you can.

Examples:

Name | Katakana | Romaji pronunciation  
---|---|---  
Ana | アナ | Ana  
Jeremy | ジェレミー | Jeremii  
Maria | マリア | Maria  
Sam | サム | Samu  
Taylor | テイラー | Teiraa  
  
If you are not sure how to write your name in katakana, use the closest version you can find and include romaji pronunciation. You can also explain your choice in one sentence.

## Useful phrase bank

Use only the phrases you need.

Japanese | Romaji pronunciation | Meaning  
---|---|---  
こんにちは | Konnichiwa | Hello  
はじめまして | Hajimemashite | Nice to meet you for the first time  
わたしは … です | Watashi wa … desu | I am …  
… からきました | … kara kimashita | I am from …  
どうぞよろしくおねがいします | Douzo yoroshiku onegai shimasu | Polite closing when meeting someone  
ありがとうございます | Arigatou gozaimasu | Thank you  
  
## Model introduction
    
    
    はじめまして。
    Hajimemashite.
    
    わたしは アナ です。
    Watashi wa Ana desu.
    
    カリフォルニア からきました。
    Kariforunia kara kimashita.
    
    どうぞよろしくおねがいします。
    Douzo yoroshiku onegai shimasu.
    

Notice that the model includes Japanese text and romaji pronunciation. Your discussion post will use the same side-by-side format.
