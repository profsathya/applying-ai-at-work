# Favorite NBA Team Discussion

Choose one NBA team you enjoy following, want to learn more about, or find interesting as a sports organization.

Your post should go beyond “I like this team.” Use the team as a way to talk about basketball style, fan identity, or what makes a team memorable.

## Initial post

Write 200 to 300 words addressing the following:

  1. Which NBA team did you choose?
  2. What drew you to that team?
  3. What is one thing the team is known for?
  4. What is one question you still have about the team’s style, history, roster, or fan culture?



You may choose a team because of a player, a city, a logo, a family connection, a rivalry, a memorable game, or simple curiosity. The reason does not need to be complicated. It does need to be specific.

## Replies

Reply to two peers.

In each reply, do one of the following:

  * Ask a follow-up question about why they chose that team.
  * Compare their team’s identity with the identity of another NBA team.
  * Share one observation about the team’s style of play, history, or fan culture.



Each reply should be at least 75 words. Avoid replies that only say you agree or like the same team. Give the other person something they can respond to.

## Evaluation

Credit is based on specificity, not expertise. A strong post names a team, gives a concrete reason for choosing it, and connects that reason to something about basketball or team identity. Strong replies extend the conversation rather than closing it.
