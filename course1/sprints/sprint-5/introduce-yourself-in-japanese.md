# Introduce Yourself in Japanese

Post a short self-introduction in Japanese. Use hiragana and katakana where you can, and include romaji pronunciation beside each Japanese line so classmates can read it.

Your post should include:

  * A greeting.
  * Your name, preferably in katakana if that fits your name.
  * One simple detail, such as where you are from or something you like.
  * A polite closing.
  * Romaji pronunciation under or beside each Japanese line.



## Example format
    
    
    はじめまして。
    Hajimemashite.
    
    わたしは アナ です。
    Watashi wa Ana desu.
    
    カリフォルニア からきました。
    Kariforunia kara kimashita.
    
    どうぞよろしくおねがいします。
    Douzo yoroshiku onegai shimasu.
    

## Reply to one peer

Reply to one peer after you post. In your reply:

  * Greet them by name.
  * Identify one kana, word, or phrase you recognized.
  * Ask one friendly follow-up question in English.



Keep the tone supportive. Everyone is practicing a new writing system.
