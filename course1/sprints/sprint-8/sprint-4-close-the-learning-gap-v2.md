# Sprint 4: Close the Learning Gap (V2)

In this sprint, you find out what you don't know that's holding the problem up. You name the gap, design a way to close it, learn the thing, and check what you learned against something that isn't AI.

This sprint is part of Reframing Problems with AI. You will keep using the same real current problem throughout the course. A workplace problem is ideal, but the problem can also come from a job search, role transition, volunteer role, community work, caregiving or family system, campus work, professional portfolio, small business, or another real setting where actual people are affected.

By the end of this sprint, you should be able to:

1. Name the gap in what you know that is blocking your next move, and tell it apart from what you are curious about.
2. Design a focused learning path with AI and sources that are not AI.
3. Check an important claim against evidence and say what the check returned.

## Sprint Rhythm

1. Read the Introduction and pass the concept check.
2. Design your learning path with the Dojo, and decide what you are not going to learn.
3. Do the learning, and check the two claims your frame rests on.
4. Update your Goal Plan and reflect on what you can now do.

## Portfolio Thread

Carry forward the work from this sprint into your living course portfolio. Keep the artifacts, AI prompts, pushback notes, stakeholder evidence, and reflections that show how your thinking changed.

## Completion Criteria

You are done when you can name what you didn't know at the start of the sprint, point at evidence that you now know it well enough to act, say what you checked it against, and show how that moves your readiness on the problem.
