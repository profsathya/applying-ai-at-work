# Sprint 3 Reflection and Mid-Course Goal Plan Revision

## Purpose

Pause at mid-course to name what stakeholder evidence changed and how your Goal Plan should adapt.

## Task Instructions

1. Review your stakeholder map, your stakeholder conversation report, and your revised problem statement.
2. Compare what AI anticipated with what a real person actually helped you see.
3. Write an initial reflection and Goal Plan revision in the activity.
4. Generate AI follow-up questions and use them to identify what is still vague, generic, or unsupported.
5. Revise your reflection so the stakeholder evidence and Goal Plan change are clear.
6. Use the deeper AI guidance button if your answer smooths over tension or conflict.
7. Save the refined response in the activity.

## Deliverable

Your refined reflection should answer:

- What a real person helped you see that AI did not.
- What part of your frame changed.
- What tension or conflict you are carrying forward.
- Your revised Goal Plan for the rest of the course.

## Submission Format

Download the JSON response file from the activity and upload it to Canvas.

## Evidence Of Success

Your final response should be specific enough that another person can understand your context, your reasoning, and what you are ready to do next. Do not submit a polished AI answer without showing your own judgment.

## Portfolio Capture

Save this as the mid-course version of your Goal Plan.
