# Course 1 Outcomes

High-level outcomes for Course 1: Problem Framing with AI. These describe what a completer can do.

- Identify AI-relevant problems in their own workplace, distinguishing genuine opportunities from situations where AI adds no value.
- Apply structured thinking to move from a vague workplace concern to a clearly framed problem with defined scope, stakeholders, and success criteria.
- Validate problem framing with real stakeholders, incorporating feedback and adjusting the frame based on what they learn from actual conversations.
- Articulate the human judgment involved in problem framing — what they brought to the process that AI could not provide, including organizational context, stakeholder relationships, and prioritization decisions.

How these outcomes are taught and assessed is for the team to design.
