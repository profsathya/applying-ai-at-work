# What already exists

Make a copy of this document for Sprint 2. Two activities use it: What solutions already exist, where you fill what you can on your own, and Dojo Lab: explore what already exists, where you fill the rest with AI and by looking for yourself. Paste the table into each activity's response box when it asks.

Work in two passes. First everything you can think of for column 1; then, one row at a time, columns 2 and 3 together. Things you know of, guesses, and questions all count, and blanks are fine. A table that is mostly questions is a good start. You are listing what exists, not designing anything.

**Column 1, what exists.** What products or services claim to solve this? Does anyone with a similar problem seem to handle it well? Does your own organization, or do you personally, already have something for this, used or not? Could AI help with it? Questions about things that might exist count as rows.

**Column 2, how it works, as far as you know.** Have you seen it yourself? Where does it live? Who can change it, who can see it, who would have to switch it on? If you have not seen it, how do you know it exists? Say what you do not know.

**Column 3, what it means for your problem.** Reasons it would be good for your problem, reasons it might be bad, and what you are not sure about. Before you have looked into anything, most of this will be "not sure." After the Dojo Lab, write it from what you found where you looked, in your words. AI can help you find out how something works; it cannot know what it means for you.
