# Welcome video, v0.1

About 520 words, which is three and a half minutes at a conversational pace. Record together on Zoom, speaker view, one take straight through with one restart allowed. Small stumbles stay in. Jeremy trims the ends and adds the final text frame.

Square brackets mark things to fill in or decide. Everything else is a suggestion to rewrite in your own voice. Keep each turn under 30 seconds.

## 1. Welcome (Tacey, 30 seconds)

Hi, and welcome to Reframing Problems with AI. I'm Tacey [last name], and I'm your instructor for this course. [One sentence about Tacey: what she teaches at De Anza, or why she took this course on.]

Here is what the next ten weeks are about. You are going to pick one real problem from your own work or life, and take it as far as knowing whether it is worth solving, who it affects, what is already being done about it, and what you would need to learn to do something about it. You will use AI the whole way through, and you will learn where it helps and where you have to do the thinking yourself.

I didn't build this course alone. Three people designed and built it with me, and you will be meeting them in office hours and one-on-ones. Let me hand over so they can introduce themselves.

## 2. The course team (25 seconds each)

**Leslie.** I'm Leslie Kern. I wrote the first two sprints, the ones where you find your problem and decide whether it holds up. [One sentence of why: what in this course matters to you.] If you are stuck in weeks one to four, I am probably the person who wrote the page you are stuck on, so come and tell me.

**Melisa.** I'm Melisa Villafuerte Mendoza. I wrote Sprints 3 and 4, where you go and talk to the people your problem affects, and work out what you still need to learn. [One sentence of why.] [One sentence about what she wants learners to get from those sprints.]

**Jeremy.** I'm Jeremy [last name]. I built the Dojo, the AI workspace you will use in every sprint, and the pages you will be working in. [One sentence of why.] If something on a page does not work the way it says it should, that is mine, and I want to hear about it.

## 3. The first-run message (Tacey, with Leslie picking up, 50 seconds)

**Tacey.** One thing we want to say plainly. This is the first time this course has run. The subject, working with AI, changes month to month, and there was no course like this to copy from. We built it carefully, and we are going to be adjusting it as we go, based on what we see from you.

**Leslie.** That means you are the first group through it, and what you tell us changes the course. If a page is confusing, if an activity takes three times longer than we said, if the AI does something strange, we want to know before you have spent an evening on it. The fastest way to tell us is to come to office hours or book a one-on-one. Those conversations are how we find out what is actually happening, and honestly, they are the part of this we are most looking forward to.

## 4. How to reach us, and where to start (Tacey, 30 seconds)

Office hours are [day and time, and the Zoom link is in Canvas under Help and Resources]. One-on-ones: [how to book, for example a link in Canvas or a message through Canvas Inbox]. For anything else, use Canvas Inbox and we will get back to you within [a day, or whatever the team commits to].

Start with Sprint 0. It is short: it sets up your AI workspace and walks you through how the course runs. Then Sprint 1 opens on [date].

## 5. Close (all, 15 seconds)

[One line each, or Tacey alone.] We are glad you are here. See you in office hours.

## Final text frame (Jeremy adds in the edit)

Office hours: [days and times]. Book a one-on-one: [link]. Questions: Canvas Inbox. Start here: Sprint 0.

## Notes

- Tacey is the instructor; the other three are the course team. Say it that way and do not explain the organisations. Learners need to know who they will talk to, not the org chart.
- Say the first-run message once, in section 3, and do not apologise elsewhere. Confidence plus honesty, not hedging.
- Once the video exists, the Sprint 0 Welcome page needs a line pointing to it, and Help and Resources needs the office-hour times and the one-on-one booking method. Those pages are the team's, so that is a note for Jeremy and Sathya.
- Office-hour times go on the text frame, not only in speech, so they can be re-cut without re-recording.
