# Course 1: Problem Framing with AI

## Purpose

Build the capability to frame a real workplace problem in a way that makes it solvable — with or without AI. Problem framing is the foundation: if you frame the wrong problem, even the best AI solution will not help. Working professionals already know their organizations; this course teaches them to use that knowledge to identify and articulate problems worth solving.

## Audience

Working professionals whose roles are being reshaped by AI. See `context/audience.md` for the full audience definition.

## Format

10 weeks, online, asynchronous.

## Structure

- **Week 1:** Orientation — meet the cohort, understand expectations, choose a workplace problem, identify initial stakeholders
- **Weeks 2–9:** Four two-week sprints developing problem framing capability
- **Week 10:** Capstone — demonstrate problem framing to peers and stakeholders

See `structure.md` for the prescribed course shape and `outcomes.md` for high-level learning outcomes.

## Outcome focus

Participants leave this course able to take a vague workplace concern and turn it into a clearly framed problem with real stakeholder validation. They can articulate why the problem matters, who it affects, and what makes it tractable — and they can explain the human judgment involved in that framing.

## Module content

Detailed sprint content is intentionally not in this README. The team will develop sprint themes, activities, assessments, and weekly briefings in `modules/`. The course structure is prescribed; the content within that structure is for the team to design.
