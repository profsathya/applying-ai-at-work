Sprint 1 is live and was built from documents, so nine of its ten pages carry a source sidecar. Rebuilt pages drop theirs, as Melisa's concept check did (Leslie, 27 September). ## The decisions that carry from Sprint 2 and 3

1. **The Introduction shape.** Title case; the sprint's own learning goal on the module header; the route as a numbered list with points; the submission line; no How to work or Where to write sections. Key terms stay where a term is a word with another meaning or a course-specific meaning; each Sprint 1 entry gets that test.
2. **Every activity page opens the same way.** A "What you need for this activity" heading and list; the Canvas paragraph; the goal paragraph on its own; then a heading where the work starts.
2a. **AI feedback on by default** (Leslie, 27 September): the walkthrough's per-entry "Get AI feedback" button, which Sprints 3 and 4 already use, is on wherever the learner has written first. Sprint 1: on for Get underneath and First frames, off for the brainstorm's first pass (Jeremy's interleaved page carries its own list feedback). Each checkpoint is registered on Jeremy's feedback service. Sprint 2's walk pages, which switched it off by design, follow once the team confirms; the Introductions' About AI paragraphs change from "without AI" to "your thinking first, then feedback."
3. **A learning goal on every page**, reading pages included. On walkthroughs it shows as the purpose line; making it catch the eye is the renderer (list item 15).
4. **Walkthroughs for one-sitting structured work**, in the grid form where the work is a table: short column headers, a Guidance row, one or two example rows (one mostly blank), numbered steps above the table, avoid lines in the criteria. Word download, uploaded in Canvas, as Jeremy's release set them.
5. **The Dojo Lab in the Sprint 2 shape.** What you need; the three parts; Part 1 one line, "Open your Dojo and say: Walk me through Dojo Lab: test, widen, choose"; the More help fallback paste; Part 2 the five transcript steps; Part 3 what to update afterwards. Jeremy's Dojo Labs tab already carries the Sprint 1 method, so the module text is his.
6. **The Problem Frame document from the start**, whole, with parts marked by the sprint and week that fill them; Part 6 as the five-column table with the Status column blank; 3c present and marked. One copy, made once, at the Problem Frame item.
7. **Document names bold at first mention** (course-wide list item 30, applied on Sprint 2 as the first case).
8. **Vocabulary.** Confirmed or unverified stays (list item 13 is the team's).
9. **The submission block, the finish block, and module order** are Jeremy's (29b, 32, 31); the pages do not work around them.

## The Candidate Log

Today it is one document with four parts: A the brainstorm, B the table underneath, C the draft frames, D the chosen frame. With walkthroughs, A, B, and C become Word files the learner downloads from each activity and uploads in Canvas. D becomes the Problem Frame document itself. So the log goes, and each page names what it needs from the previous page's Word file. What is lost: one place holding the whole sprint. What is gained: no template to copy at the start, and the same shape as Sprint 2.

Open question for Leslie: whether the learner is told to keep the three Word files together somewhere (a folder, the working book question from list item 33), or whether each activity simply needs the previous file open.
