## Part B. What already exists

Activity: What could already solve this, then Dojo Lab: explore what already exists. Start the table on your own before you open a chat. Things you know of, guesses, and questions all count. Blanks are fine; a table that is mostly questions is a good start. You are listing what exists, not designing anything.

If nothing comes to mind, try these: Is there a product for this? Is there a name for this kind of problem? How do people who do this well do it? Does anyone in your own organisation already handle it? What happens in places with the same problem and more money, or less?
