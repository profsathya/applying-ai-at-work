# Instructor Notes

This is a self-paced course. It should not require a live lecture, external reading, or a walkthrough.

## Facilitation Role

The facilitator or reviewer should:

- Confirm learners have repo access.
- Confirm whether the learner is authorized to use Canvas credentials.
- Keep Canvas publishing separate from drafting.
- Review applied tasks for safe scoping and evidence of verification.
- Help non-technical learners interpret validation or diff output without turning the course into a command-line course.

## What To Watch For

- Learners may ask Codex to "just push it" before reviewing local Markdown.
- Learners may paste real tokens into prompts or files.
- Learners may confuse `course3/manifests/production.json` with the human-readable Canvas mapping files.
- Learners may think schema validation proves instructional quality. It proves structure, not quality.
- Learners may assume Canvas edits automatically come back into local Markdown. They do not.

## Review Standards

A successful learner submission should show:

- A scoped Codex prompt.
- A clear statement of what files are in scope.
- A clear statement of what must not change.
- Evidence that Codex inspected relevant repo files.
- Local validation or a documented reason validation was not run.
- A diff or file review checklist.
- A Canvas safety decision.

## Optional Cohort Use

For a cohort, ask learners to share one prompt and one review finding from their applied task. Do not require them to share secrets, `.env` values, Canvas tokens, or private course URLs.
