## Course Purpose

Career Intelligence helps participants build a defensible niche and then run the job search as an evidence-based weekly cycle. The course should keep participants grounded in real postings, real professional conversations, and artifacts that can be reviewed by another person.

## Audience And Situation

The audience is graduating seniors or near-graduates preparing for a labor market being reshaped by AI. Participants may have uneven job-search fluency, uneven AI fluency, and different levels of clarity about the roles they are pursuing.

## Course Arc

Weeks 1-3 focus on Know Your Niche: participants identify what they bring, test it against market evidence, and defend a niche that is more specific than a broad domain. Weeks 4-6 focus on Own Your Search: participants run search hypotheses, revise resume artifacts, initiate professional interactions, and synthesize what changed.

## Sprint / Module Map

### Sprint 0: Week 1 - Orientation and Know Your Niche

- Orient to the program.
- Submit current resume and baseline pre-evaluation.
- Build early peer connection.
- Start the Niche Doc.

### Sprint 1: Week 2 - Develop and Articulate Your Niche

- Test the current resume against the niche.
- Analyze 10 real job postings.
- Revise resume and Niche Doc.

### Sprint 2: Week 3 - Verify Your Niche and Begin Own Your Search

- Verify the niche with another person.
- Submit Know Your Niche artifact v1.
- Begin the Search Strategy Hypothesis.

### Sprint 3: Week 4 - Iteration 1

- Run the first search cycle or sharpen the niche.
- Submit weekly hypothesis and reflection.
- Tailor one resume version for one role type.

### Sprint 4: Week 5 - Iteration 2 and Professional Interaction

- Prepare external professional interaction.
- Initiate one outreach, networking, or industry conversation.
- Interpret signal from reply or silence.

### Sprint 5: Week 6 - Iteration 3, External Signal, and Synthesis

- Use external signal and course artifacts to synthesize the search direction.
- Submit final tailored resume and fit explanation.
- Complete program synthesis self-check.

## Assessment Strategy

Assignments should emphasize evidence, specificity, and concrete next moves. Discussions should create peer pressure-testing rather than role-play. Self-checks may be zero-point assignments when they serve as completion gates or reflection checkpoints.
