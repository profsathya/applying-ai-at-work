# Certificate Overview

**External name:** Applying AI at Work: Build Solutions with a Human Touch

**Format:** Online, asynchronous, two 10-week courses

**Partnership:** CTI provides the curriculum design and frameworks. De Anza College provides delivery infrastructure and accreditation. The intent is to scale to additional community colleges after the initial offering.

**Target launch:** Fall 2026

## Value proposition

Your employees already know your business. We teach them to direct AI with that knowledge.

## Who this is for

Working professionals whose roles are being reshaped by AI. They have real jobs, real stakeholders, and existing knowledge of their organizations. This certificate builds AI direction capability on top of that knowledge. See `context/audience.md` for a fuller audience definition.

## Two-course structure

- **Course 1: Problem Framing with AI** — Build the capability to take a vague workplace concern and turn it into a clearly framed problem, validated by real stakeholders.
- **Course 2: Building Solutions with AI** — Build the capability to direct AI in solving a framed problem, with the participant providing judgment, context, and accountability.

Each course is 10 weeks: Week 1 orientation + 4 two-week sprints + Week 10 capstone. This shape is load-bearing for SDT: orientation creates the autonomy moment (participants choose their problem), the capstone creates the competence moment (participants demonstrate what they can now do). The sprint cadence provides rhythm without micromanaging weekly deliverables.

## What a certificate completer can do

- Identify where AI can address real operational problems in their own organization, using their existing knowledge of the business to distinguish genuine opportunities from hype.
- Frame problems correctly: moving from a vague workplace concern to a clearly scoped problem statement validated by real stakeholders.
- Build or direct working AI-augmented solutions, articulating what the human contributes (judgment, context, accountability) and what AI contributes (scale, speed, breadth).
