# Open Questions

Things the team needs to decide. These are unresolved by design — the repository captures the structure and philosophy, but these decisions require team discussion.

- What is the end-of-course capstone for each course? What does the Week 10 deliverable look like?
- What happens during the Week 1 orientation specifically? What activities, introductions, and problem-selection mechanics are involved?
- How do peer cohorts work in an asynchronous format? What structures create real peer interaction without requiring synchronous attendance?
- How are real stakeholders documented and verified? What evidence does a participant provide that they actually talked to someone?
- What delivery platform will be used? Canvas, a custom platform, or something else?
- How is AI partnership made visible in specific sprint mechanics? What activities make the human and AI contributions distinct and identifiable?
- What is the cohort size assumption? How many participants per cohort, and how does that affect peer interaction design?
- How is the train-the-trainer handoff structured for partner colleges? What do instructors at other institutions need to deliver this certificate effectively?
- What kind of stakeholder engagement is appropriate for each sprint, given the participant's workplace context? How does the expected depth of engagement change across the course arc?
