# Career Intelligence

This local course shell maps Career Intelligence to Canvas course ID `183` through `course5/manifests/production.json`.

The course is source-backed for generated hosted homepages and progress-aware Canvas launch. Course content lives under `course5/sprints/`, while `course5/homepage.yaml` contains optional curated homepage copy and grouping.

Nothing in this directory should be pushed to Canvas without the normal validation and production publish workflow.

## Course Map

| Module | Path | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1: Orientation and Know Your Niche | `course5/sprints/sprint-0/` | Orient to the program, connect with peers, and start a niche document. |
| Week 2: Develop and Articulate Your Niche | `course5/sprints/sprint-1/` | Test the resume and job market against the current niche. |
| Week 3: Verify Your Niche and Begin Own Your Search | `course5/sprints/sprint-2/` | Defend the niche with evidence and begin the search strategy hypothesis. |
| Week 4: Iteration 1 | `course5/sprints/sprint-3/` | Run the first search cycle or sharpen the niche before searching. |
| Week 5: Iteration 2 and Professional Interaction | `course5/sprints/sprint-4/` | Move from private preparation to external professional signal. |
| Week 6: Iteration 3, External Signal, and Synthesis | `course5/sprints/sprint-5/` | Synthesize evidence, search direction, and next development moves. |
