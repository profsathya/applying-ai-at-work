The Challenge

Generic AI Training Doesn't Develop Judgment

AI is reshaping every job. Most training teaches people to use AI tools — but tool familiarity is not the same as the judgment to direct AI in service of real work. The people who create value in an AI-augmented economy are the ones who can frame the right problems, bring institutional knowledge to bear, and direct AI toward outcomes that matter. That capability is not in the tool. It is in the person.

Your employees already know your business. We teach them to direct AI with that knowledge.

Theoretical Foundation

Self-Determination Theory as Design Principle

Three Psychological Needs

Autonomy

Participants choose their own problem from their own workplace. They are not all working the same scenario.

Competence

Visible, calibrated evidence of growing capability throughout the course — not just at the end.

Relatedness

Real human connection — real stakeholders, real peers. Not simulated, not AI-roleplayed.

How It Works

Two Courses, Real Problems, Real Stakeholders

Each course is 10 weeks. Week 1 is orientation — participants choose their problem, meet their cohort, and set the terms of their work. Weeks 2 through 9 are four two-week sprints, each building capability through real work on real problems. Week 10 is a capstone where participants demonstrate their work to peers and stakeholders. Throughout, participants engage real stakeholders in their organizations and develop the meta-habits and capabilities to direct AI in service of value that matters.

Meta-Habits — How You Show Up

Slow Down

Make space for thinking before acting. Speed without judgment produces volume, not value.

Know Yourself

Understand your assumptions, strengths, and gaps. Know what you bring that AI does not.

Take the Lead

Direct AI rather than be led by it. Own the outcomes and the human consequences.

Capabilities — What You Develop

Self-Directed Learner

Identify what you don't know, design how to learn it, use AI as a learning partner.

Integrative Solver

Synthesize across stakeholders and perspectives to find solutions that hold.

Adaptive Builder

Test assumptions, build prototypes, pivot on evidence, iterate toward working solutions.

The foundational layer: reading closely, writing to think, listening actively, and talking to test ideas — the mechanisms of thought itself.
