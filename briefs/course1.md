## Constraints (non-negotiable)

- 10-week shape: Week 1 orientation + four two-week sprints + Week 10 capstone.
- Every sprint has at least one real stakeholder touchpoint.
- Every sprint produces a visible capability artifact.
- Every sprint delivers value immediately (not promised for later).
- Frameworks shape what participants do; framework names do not appear in participant-facing body text.
- Voice is direct, respects time, assumes professional judgment.
