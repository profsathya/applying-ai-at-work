# Brief: Course 2 (Building Solutions with AI)

Plan Course 2 of the "Applying AI at Work" certificate. Course 2 builds on Course 1 but can be taken independently.

## Authoritative sources

- `course2/design/README.md` - course purpose and format
- `course2/design/structure.md` - 10-week shape and sprint constraints
- `course2/design/outcomes.md` - four high-level outcomes

## Design philosophy (read before planning)

Same as Course 1 (see `briefs/course1.md`). The framework and design-principle docs apply to both courses.

## Additional Course-2-specific consideration

Course 2 emphasizes the **AI partnership moment** in every sprint: a structured activity where the human and AI contributions are distinct and identifiable. See `context/ai-partnership.md`. Plan at least one "partnership visibility" artifact per sprint.

## Produce

- `course2/prd.json`
- `course2/course.yaml`
- `course2/progress.md`
- `course2/manifests/production.json`

## Constraints (non-negotiable)

- 10-week shape: Week 1 orientation + four two-week sprints + Week 10 capstone.
- Every sprint has at least one real stakeholder touchpoint.
- Every sprint has a visible AI partnership moment.
- Every sprint produces a visible capability artifact.
- Frameworks shape what participants do; framework names do not appear in participant-facing body text.
